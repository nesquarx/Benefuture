[[Category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]] [[category:WorldBuilding]]
A benesophont or sophont in [[benefuture]] is a [[person]] whose [[interaction]]s are all aligned with the [[benefuture]] [[goals]] and [[philosophy]]. That is, their [[output]]s consistently forward [[understanding]] and then [[happiness]]; beliefs and preferences are not directly accessible, so alignment is read from outputs.

==Aspects==
* A benesophont [[understanding|understands]] the [[benefuture]] [[philosophy]].
* A benesophont is capable of sufficient [[sentience]] and [[sapience]] as a person to be able to actively apply the benefuture goals to align their interactions.
* A benesophont abides by the [[law]]s of benefuture as a direct consequence of the law's utility and not because the laws hold any other special meaning.
* A benesophont utilises and exercises their [[rights]], understands and enjoys their [[law#Privileges|privileges]], performs their [[law#Duties|Duties]] and attempts to perform their [[law#Responsibilities|responsibilities]], all as long as it works towards the [[goals]].
* An aspiring benesophont is expected to use their [[Person#Aspects|personal capacities]] to intend and attempt rational action towards the forwarding goals, but the benefuture [[system design]] does not assume them to succeed and thus accounts for irrational actions which can then be nudged towards rationality to bring their intents and attempts to fruition.
* Sophoncy is not a higher privileged class over a non benefuture Aligned [[person]] (who are still fully protected as all beings are in Benefuture), but it's definitely a more responsible class. To have control over the mechanisms of benefuture, a track record of goal-aligned outputs ([[reputation]]) is necessary. Alignment is with the goals, not with any implementation mechanism; dissent over mechanisms is channelled through [[Adversarial adaptation]].

==Discussion==
===Moral agency, practical wisdom, and substrate independence===
Science fiction and philosophy of mind adopted the term ''sophont'' (coined by Poul Anderson from Greek ''sophos'', wise) to name any being possessing reasoning intelligence regardless of biological or synthetic substrate. Benefuture narrows the civic category to ''benesophont'': a [[person]] whose [[sapience]], [[sentience]], and [[morality]] all clear the [[threshold of significance]]. This mirrors Aristotle's distinction between mere cleverness (''deinotes'', instrumental skill in achieving any target) and practical wisdom (''phronesis'', deliberation aligned with the flourishing of the whole community),<ref>Aristotle (c. 340 BCE). [https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.6.vi.html ''Nicomachean Ethics'', Book VI, Chapter 12 (1144a)]. Translated by W. D. Ross.</ref> as well as Rawls's pairing of the ''rational'' (capacity to pursue a conception of the good) with the ''reasonable'' (readiness to propose and abide by fair terms of cooperation).<ref>Rawls, J. (1993). [https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2009261W ''Political Liberalism''] (Lecture II, §1, pp. 48–54). Columbia University Press.</ref>

Crucially, because internal mental states cannot be directly inspected across diverse biological and artificial minds, Benefuture evaluates benesophont standing strictly through observable [[output]]s—analogous to Samuelson's revealed preference criterion in economics,<ref>Samuelson, P. A. (1938). [https://doi.org/10.2307/2548836 A Note on the Pure Theory of Consumer's Behaviour]. ''Economica'', 5(17), 61–71.</ref> applied here to demonstrated harm avoidance and positive contribution.
