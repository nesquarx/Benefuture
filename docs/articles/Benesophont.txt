A benesophont or sophont in [[benefuture]] is a [[person]] whose [[interaction]]s are all aligned with the [[benefuture]] [[goals]] and [[philosophy]]. That is, their [[preference]]s are to maximise [[understanding]] and then [[happiness]].

==Aspects==
* A benesophont [[understanding|understands]] the [[benefuture]] [[philosophy]].
* A benesophont is capable of sufficient [[sentience]] and [[sapience]] as a person to be able to actively apply the benefuture goals to align their interactions.
* A benesophont abides by the [[law]]s of benefuture as a direct consequence of the law's utility and not because the laws hold any other special meaning.
* A benesophont utilises and exercises their [[rights]], understands and enjoys their [[law#Privileges|privileges]], performs their [[law#Duties|Duties]] and attempts to perform their [[law#Responsibilities|responsibilities]], all as long as it works towards the [[goals]].
* An aspiring benesophont is expected to use their [[Person#Aspects|personal capacities]] to intend and attempt rational action towards the forwarding goals, but the benefuture [[system design]] does not assume them to succeed and thus accounts for irrational actions which can then be nudged towards rationality to bring their intents and attempts to fruition.
* Sophoncy is not a higher privileged class over a non benefuture Aligned [[person]] (who are still fully protected as all beings are in Benefuture), but it's definitely a more responsible class. To have control over the mechanisms of benefuture, alignment is necessary.
