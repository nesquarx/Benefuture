The system of channels of [[communication]] and [[interaction]]s between [[person]]s, [[being]]s and [[entities]] in a [[society]] are called [[social networks]]. [[Benefuture]] social networks are focused on making the channels of communication such that they facilitate [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]] in the [[Life in benefuture|lives of sophonts]] and thus work towards the [[benefuture goals]].

== Aspects ==

* Amongst human societies, communication is generally achieved via the following means:
** '''Verbal''' - using symbolic language 
** '''Nonverbal''' - emotions, actions, etc.
* The channels used for communication are:
** '''Physical''' - speech, gestures, expressions, writing and art as [[Thing|things]] in [[reality]] directly from one [[person]] or [[being]] to another.
** '''Digital''' - the internet and electronic media, used to record, store, transmit and replay the physical communication equivalents.
** '''Transactional''' - actions, events, purchases, gifts, and any significant [[interaction]] in the [[System Design#Behavioral Transaction Substrate|behavioural substrate]] of the participants of society.
*Of these, the internet as a nascent global network for communication has become a major network for sharing understanding and generating happiness, but it is not optimized for such. To optimize it, we would need:
**Establishment of degree and quality [[truth]] in the transmitted communications.
**Prioritizing the sharing of [[Truth|truths]] which increase understanding and/or happiness and decrease [[ignorance]] or harm.
*This might mean newer methods of chain-of-source, chain-of-veracity and benefuture goal calculation artefacts inserted into communication to properly contextualize information, such as:
**Trace its source and mutations.
**Categorize it and its subcomponents into one or more types such as: identified malicious disinformation / rigorously falsified misinformation / tentatively falsified misinformation / information under debate / fiction / opinion / wild speculation / tentative speculation / tentatively unfalsified fact / rigorously unfalsified fact / formally proven statement - etc.
**Establish its degree and level of verification and dispute.
**Calculate its understanding impact and happiness impact.
*This might mean a different prioritization in the dissemination of news, entertainment and [[education]] through social networks.
*This might mean newer methods of quantifying, qualifying and weighing information passed through the networks on the basis of happiness and understanding generated and distributing [[currency]] accordingly.
*As a key system of  [[benefuture]] [[civilization]], [[Anti-Corruption|anti-corruption]] measures and [[Progressive complexity|Progressive strategies]] also need to be applied in its [[system design]].

== Example ==

* Modern social networks exist in multiple formats but is not optimized for benefuture.
* A [[benefuture currency]] [[Technology Network|technology network]] is a network optimized for benefuture [[society]].
