Enforcement is to influence the [[output]]s of all [[person]]s toward the [[goals]] of the [[society]], sometimes against their immediate will. Enforcement takes mandatory [[law]]s as the only mandate for force, and the [[goals]] as paramount in everything else.

==Aspects==
* The most effective method of enforcement is via design, that is, to create the structures, mechanisms and laws of society such that:
** Optimal goal forwarding actions are easier to take and encouraged by the [[administration]] via [[accolade]]s.
** Suboptimal or goal-negative actions are more difficult to take and are discouraged via punitive measures such as providing [[Justice#Reparation|reparation]] and undergoing [[Justice#Rehabilitation|rehabilitation]].
* The next effective method is via forcible influence:
** When enforcement encounters [[person]]s providing [[output]]s that clearly violate mandatory [[law]]s, persuasive influence is applied first and then forcible influence, and subsequent processes follow [[justice]].
** [[Output]]s that are lawful but goal-negative are met by design, pricing and persuasion, and by law updates triggered through [[Adversarial adaptation]], never by force.
** When [[justice]] prescribes reparations and rehabilitations, enforcement needs to influence all the persons involved to undergo them, persuasively or forcibly.
* The balance of design, persuasion, and force is delicate. The power to do so can be easily abused and must be kept under strict restraint.
* Enforcement is often a separate part of the administration that the part which makes the laws, as a measure against concentration of power. It should fall under the same scrutiny as other powerful entities and [[Anti-Corruption]] measures should be applied.
* As a key system of  [[benefuture]] [[civilization]], [[Progressive complexity|Progressive strategies]] need to be applied in its design.


==Examples==
* For most governments, their enforcement arms are of a policing nature, benefuture's enforcement expectations are more widespread and design oriented than policing oriented.
* In parenting, there are measures of enforcement which tries to enforce eusocial goals into children's behaviours. It can also go wrong in the same ways societal enforcement can.
