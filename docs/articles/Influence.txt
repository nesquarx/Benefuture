[[category:Benefuture]][[category:Definitions]]
Influence is the ability of any entity to control and direct the [[Output|outputs]] of other entities.

== Aspects ==
Influence is usually achieved via manipulation of an entity's inputs to modify their [[understanding]] of [[reality]] which makes the influenced output the best choice.

* When the influence is improving the quality of understanding the entity has of reality and improving their understanding and happiness over time, it is a [[good]] influence. Or else it's a [[bad]] influence.

=== Examples ===

* Influence using [[communication]]
** Via persuasion, with promises of gains.
** Via coercion, with promises of losses.
** Via false information which affect their decisions.
** Via partial [[Truth|truths]] which affect their decisions.
*Influence using physical means
**Restricting an entity's options leaving only the influenced output as the viable choice
**Altering the reality around an entity to affect their decisions
*Influence via leverage
**Via affecting or promise to affect [[Thing|things]] that the entity values highly (people and possessions)
