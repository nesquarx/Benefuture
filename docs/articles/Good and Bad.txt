'''Good''' is an umbrella term for [[Outcome|outcomes]] gainful or positive to an [[Entity|entity's]] [[goals]]. 

'''Bad''' is an umbrella term for outcomes lossful or negative to the goals. 

In [[benefuture]] terms, '''good''' [[Output|outputs]] and [[Outcome|outcomes]] increase personal or universal [[understanding]] or [[happiness]]. '''bad''' outputs and outcomes decrease those. Whatever increases them optimally, is '''best'''. Whatever decreases them the most, is '''worst'''.

== Aspects ==

* Goodness or badness are subjective to the entity, hence any mention of good or bad does need the context of the subject for whom it is good or bad.
* Generally, a notion of objective or universal good or bad is meaningless without the context, in the context of benefuture, the subject is all known beings and the goals are the [[benefuture goals]].
* To speak of a theoretical quantitative process, the calculated value of good for an output or outcome as measured against it not being performed or observed, is the numerical outcome the utility function of V discussed in the goals.
