Power is the magnitude of capacity to affect changes upon [[reality]], [[thing]]s, and [[entities]] via [[influence]], [[law]], currency, information, energy, etc.

==Aspects==
* Power is usually ascribed to [[entities]] and [[interaction]]s which can affect change.
* Power can be inherent, acquired, stable or transient, volatile, direct or indirect.
* Power can have nuance and multiple dimensions based on the nature and magnitude of change in [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]] of [[entities]] it impacts.
* Power can vary based on:
** How it has accumulated
** How frequently it can be used
** The expenditure of time, effort, energy, etc, required to use it 
** The circumstances that restrict its use
