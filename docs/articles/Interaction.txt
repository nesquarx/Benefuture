[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
Interaction is the process of a [[thing]] (interactor) taking [[input]]s and producing [[output]]s, usually from and to [[reality]], or another [[thing]] (interactee).

==Aspects==
* Input and output involves change, even the passing of pure information is not devoid of change, hence interaction changes the interactor and interactee by definition.
* Interaction is one of the major aspects of a [[being]].
* Interaction in the form of [[communication]] is one of the major aspects of a [[person]].
* For [[being]]s and [[entity|entities]], interactions can have complex chains of intent for the interactor, such as initiating an output to gain an input to be able to perform another output and so on.

==Examples==
* '''Movement''', to displace the interactor's [[Being#Body|body]], or a part of a body.
* '''Perception''', to gather information from reality about [[thing]]s.
* '''Consumption''' and '''Excretion''', to sustain a being.
* '''[[Communication]]''', to convey information to another [[entity]].

==Discussion==
===Causal processes and physical information transfer===
In the philosophy of science, Salmon distinguished genuine causal processes from pseudo-processes by their ability to transmit a conserved physical quantity or structural ''mark'' from one spacetime region to another.<ref>Salmon, W. C. (1984). [https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2796608W ''Scientific Explanation and the Causal Structure of the World''] (Chapter 5). Princeton University Press.</ref> Landauer's principle in statistical physics establishes the converse bridge for information: because information is always physically instantiated ("information is physical"), every transfer, copy, or erasure of an informational state requires a physical interaction between systems.<ref>Landauer, R. (1991). [https://doi.org/10.1063/1.881299 Information is Physical]. ''Physics Today'', 44(5), 23–29.</ref>

Benefuture treats ''interaction'' as the directed primitive connecting an acting [[entity]]'s [[output]] to a receiving [[thing]]'s [[input]]. Beyond physics, cumulative interaction weights also define the topology of a [[being]]'s [[Being#Self|extended self]] and the channels along which [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] and legal liability flow; because every interaction leaves a trace in the [[Technology Network|civilizational memory]], there are no anonymous one-shot defection games.
