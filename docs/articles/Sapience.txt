Sapience is the ability to [[understanding|understand]] any [[thing]] and maximise the accuracy of prediction of the thing [[interaction|interacting]] any other thing (especially [[reality]]).

==Aspects==
* Sapience allows an [[entity]] to predict possible futures and hypothetical scenarios.
* The degree of sapience varies widely, is multi dimensional, and does not apply equally to any [[thing]].
* Sapience is a key property of an [[entity]], hence a [[being]], hence a [[person]], hence a [[benesophont]].

==Examples==
* Sapience is often correlated to intelligence in [[being]]s.
* Problem solving ability, reasoning ability, resource management, etc. are some key outcomes of sufficient sapience.
