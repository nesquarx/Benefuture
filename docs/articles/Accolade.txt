An accolade is acknowledgement of a [[sophont]] behaving in a way that advances the [[goals]] of the [[society]] outside of their [[duty|duties]].

==Aspects==
* An accolade can be a reward: in [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] it is the weight given to the type of a contribution.
* It is a merit only when no one would have done the same unprompted; work done under contract is paid by the contract, which was worth more than not taking it.
* Accolades are designed in a way to promote more accolade worthy behaviour without gaming the system.

==Examples==
* An increase in social credit upon helping a fellow [[sophont]].
* A currency award upon adding to universal [[understanding]] that contributes to further gains.

==Discussion==
===Supererogation, marginal contribution, and Goodhart resistance===
In moral philosophy, Urmson introduced the modern analysis of ''supererogation''—actions that are praiseworthy and valuable beyond the call of strict duty, yet neither obligatory nor already compensated by prior agreement.<ref>Urmson, J. O. (1958). ''Saints and Heroes''. In A. I. Melden (Ed.), [https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4907340W ''Essays in Moral Philosophy''] (pp. 198–216). University of Washington Press.</ref> In mechanism design and cooperative game theory, rewarding an agent by their counterfactual marginal contribution—what would not have occurred had the agent not stepped in, as in the Shapley value or Vickrey–Clarke–Groves pivot mechanism—aligns individual incentives with total social value.<ref>Shapley, L. S. (1953). ''A Value for n-Person Games''. In H. W. Kuhn & A. W. Tucker (Eds.), [https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4417976W ''Contributions to the Theory of Games''] (Vol. II, pp. 307–317). Princeton University Press.</ref>

At the same time, Goodhart's law warns that any simple proxy target ceases to be a good measure once agents optimize to game it.<ref>Goodhart, C. A. E. (1984). ''Problems of Monetary Management: The U.K. Experience''. In [https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-349-17295-5_4 ''Monetary Theory and Practice''] (pp. 91–121). Palgrave Macmillan.</ref> Benefuture avoids proxy gaming by restricting accolades to uncontracted, counterfactual gains that would not have occurred unprompted (`Accolade`) and settling their weight through measured downstream outcomes in [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]].
