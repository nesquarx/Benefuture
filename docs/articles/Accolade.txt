[[category:Benefuture]]
An accolade is acknowledgement of a [[sophont]] behaving in a way that advances the [[goals]] of the [[society]] outside of their [[duty|duties]].

==Aspects==
* An accolade can be a reward: in [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] it is the weight given to the type of a contribution.
* It is a merit only when no one would have done the same unprompted; work done under contract is paid by the contract, which was worth more than not taking it.
* Accolades are designed in a way to promote more accolade worthy behaviour without gaming the system.

==Examples==
* An increase in social credit upon helping a fellow [[sophont]].
* A currency award upon adding to universal [[understanding]] that contributes to further gains.
