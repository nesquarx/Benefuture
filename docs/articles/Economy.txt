[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]] [[Category:WorldBuilding]]
An economy is the process of production, distribution, exchange and consumption of resources and value in a [[civilization]].

In a [[benefuture]] economy, value is defined to be [[understanding]] and [[happiness]], and resources are consumable [[Thing|things]] like goods, services, information and even time. They are all managed via a [[Benefuture currency]].

== Aspects ==

* We model the benefuture economy as a system which consumes resources and time, uses [[Benesophont|sophonts]] as a medium, to produce [[understanding]] and [[happiness]] for all beings in a [[civilization]].
* The major medium of defining value for exchange is [[currency]], human definitions of value in currency and the promises around it (demand, supply, debt, speculation) have largely defined our economies.<ref>Galbraith, J. K. (1975). ''Money: Whence it came, where it went''. Houghton Mifflin. https://openlibrary.org/works/OL12558W</ref>
*Traditional models initially started from exchange value, then focused on productivity, and is now often driven by fiat of speculative forces increasingly detached from [[reality]] across [[social networks]].<ref>Keen, S. (2011). ''Debunking economics: The naked emperor dethroned?'' Zed Books. https://doi.org/10.5040/9781350250963</ref><ref>Krippner, G. R. (2011). ''Capitalizing on crisis: The political origins of the rise of finance''. Harvard University Press. https://doi.org/10.4159/9780674058873</ref>
*The benefuture economic model aims to correct for this goal drift by repeatedly tuning economical forces to [[influence]] participants who can otherwise be irrational to maximize the [[benefuture goals]].
*A benefuture economy is heavily dependent upon technology and communication, but its roots lie in defining the [[benefuture currency]] to constantly map resources to the goals.
*As a key system of  [[benefuture]] [[civilization]], [[Anti-Corruption|anti-corruption]] measures and [[Progressive complexity|Progressive strategies]] also need to be applied in its [[System Design|system design]].

== Discussion ==
=== In simulation ===
* In the calibrated economy, population floors grow across every seed, and books balance under every noise source at once; with all noise sources pushed to their realistic bad end simultaneously, floors still rise at a twelfth to a fifth of clean growth ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/docs/CLAIMS.md the claims record]).
* Balance overflow trades reinvestment against concentration: the reinvested share shifts from 84% to 11% as the Gini coefficient rises from 0.18 to 0.53.
