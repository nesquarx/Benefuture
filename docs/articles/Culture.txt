Culture, in a general sense, encompasses the shared beliefs, values, customs, behaviors, and artifacts that characterize a group or subgroup of [[society]], often embedded in language, traditions, lifestyle, religion, entertainment, etc. It is transmitted through social learning and shapes the way individuals perceive and interact with the world. Within [[Benefuture]], culture is viewed as a ''memetic vehicle'' <ref name="Ermakov2021">Ermakov, D., & Ermakov, A. (2021). Memetic approach to cultural evolution. ''Biosystems'', ''204'', 104378. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystems.2021.104378</ref> – a carrier of societal habits, both beneficial and detrimental. Culture is also a powerful tool for achieving the [[Benefuture goals]].

== Aspects ==

*   '''Memetic Vehicle:''' Culture acts as a medium for transmitting patterns of thought and behavior (memes) across generations. These patterns can be beneficial, neutral, or harmful to the [[Benefuture goals]].
*   '''Forms of Expression:''' Culture manifests in various forms, including traditions, language, religion, social structures, art, and values.
*   '''Local Minima Problem:''' Cultural practices can become entrenched, even if suboptimal (local minima), resisting beneficial change due to inertia and tradition.
----
*   '''Benefuture's Approach:''' Continuous critical evaluation of all cultural practices, assessing their impact on [[Understanding]] (U), [[Happiness]] (H), and [[harm]] minimization. Beneficial practices are encouraged; harmful ones are discouraged. 
*   '''Protection of diversity:''' Crucially, [[Benefuture goals]] actively reward novel cultural expressions and approaches to achieving U/H, fostering [[diversity]] and preventing stagnation and imperialism.
*   '''Caution and Gradualism:''' Cultural change is approached with caution, respect, and sensitivity. Change is gradual, evidence-based, and driven by community consensus.
*   '''Systemic Integration:''' Actively integrated into the core systems. [[Law|Laws]] protect cultural rights and support cultural institutions. The [[economy]] and [[Benefuture currency|Benefuture Currency]] rewards activities with positive cultural impact. [[Government|Government processes]] promote participation, protect cultural heritage, and foster cultural exchange.
*    '''Novelty as a Core Value:''' The [[Benefuture goals]] incorporates a "novelty factor" in its assessment of U/H contributions. New, original, and [[Diversity|diverse]] approaches to achieving societal goals are ''inherently'' valued and rewarded.

== Examples ==

*   '''Beneficial Practices (Promoted):'''
**   Open inquiry and critical thinking.
**   Helping beings in need.
**   Collaboration and knowledge sharing.
**   Emphasis on education and lifelong learning.
**   Respect for diversity and inclusivity.
**   Sustainable practices that protect the environment.
**   Artistic expression that promotes understanding and empathy.
**   Practices that demonstrably enhance physical or mental well-being.
*   '''Harmful Practices (Discouraged/Transformed):'''
**   Practices that promote violence, discrimination, or hatred.
**   Practices that demonstrably harm physical or mental well-being
**   Superstitions that hinder rational decision-making.
**   Practices that cause significant environmental damage.
**   Rigid social hierarchies that limit individual potential.
*   '''Complex Practices (Careful Evaluation Needed):'''
**   Religious beliefs: Some aspects may promote well-being, others may be harmful. Careful analysis and open dialogue are needed.
**   Certain traditions: Traditions can have personal and social value, but the impact needs to be studied.

== Discussion ==

=== Cultural Relativism and Universal Values ===

Benefuture acknowledges cultural differences in values and practices. While respecting this diversity, Benefuture upholds the universal values of maximizing [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]] and minimizing [[harm]]. This requires a delicate balancing act, engaging in open dialogue and cross-cultural learning. The system's inherent reward for novelty provides a strong mechanism for balancing universal values with cultural diversity, incentivizing unique cultural expressions that contribute to U/H.

=== Cultural Preservation ===

Benefuture values cultural heritage and recognizes the importance of preserving traditions, languages <ref>S. Kirby, H. Cornish, & K. Smith, Cumulative cultural evolution in the laboratory: An experimental approach to the origins of structure in human language, ''Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A.'' 105 (31) 10681-10686, [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0707835105&#x20;(2008). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0707835105 (2008).]</ref>, and artistic expressions that contribute to human flourishing. However, preservation does not mean stagnation. Valuable cultural elements can be adapted and integrated into a dynamic and evolving cultural landscape.

=== Mechanism of Change ===

Cultural change is a gradual and participatory process, driven by:

*   '''[[Education]]''': Fostering critical thinking and understanding of Benefuture principles.
*   '''[[Benefuture currency|Benefuture Currency]]''': Incentivizing activities that have a positive cultural impact, *with a particular emphasis on novel contributions within these areas.* Examples include:
**   Funding for cultural projects and initiatives.
**   Rewards for individuals who contribute to cross-cultural understanding.
**   Support for the preservation of endangered languages and traditions.
*   '''[[Social networks]]''': Facilitating dialogue, debate, sharing best practices, and promoting beneficial memes.
*   '''[[Administration]]''': Playing a supporting role, facilitating cultural exchange, providing resources, and, with broad community consensus, enacting [[law]]s that discourage harmful practices.
*   '''Art:''' Art is defined as a purposive reorganization of [[reality]] by an artist intended to engineer a specific shift in audience sentience and understanding. It is a high-level [[interaction]], a [[communication]] where the artwork acts as a medium for an intended emotional affect and cross-domain information. The success of art is measured by the congruence between the artist's intent and the average audience reception. The value of Art is determined by the [[truth]]-level of the [[understanding]] it provides beyond the superficial understanding of the artwork, and the novelty of its execution. Art can be implicit where the observer creates art for themselves, including inspiration from a natural source.

=== Potential Challenges ===

*   '''Resistance to Change:''' People often resist changing ingrained beliefs and practices.
*   '''Defining "Harm":''' Determining what constitutes "harm" can be subjective.
*   '''Unintended Consequences:''' Attempts to shape culture can have unforeseen negative effects.
*   '''Cultural Imperialism:''' There's a risk of imposing a single, dominant culture. Benefuture actively guards against this through the [[Benefuture goals|Benefuture Goals]]' inherent reward for novelty and avoidance of monocultures, promoting a constant exploration of diverse approaches to achieving U/H, and the emphasis on decentralized decision making.
*   '''Backlash:''' Pushing for change too quickly can lead to resistance.
