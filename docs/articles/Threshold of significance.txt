[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
A threshold of significance is an understood and agreed upon value beyond which any metric starts affecting a decision.

==Aspects==
* The main aspect of a threshold is that it needs to be set, a choice made in consideration of all relevant factors.
* All parties using the threshold need to understand it for it to matter.
* A threshold need not be one dimensional and fixed, it can be a value varying on multiple axes dependent on relevant factors.
* A threshold can be modified upon greater [[understanding]] achieved for relevant factors and possible [[outcome]]s.
* For a sufficiently established [[benefuture]] [[society]], the main battlegrounds and concentrations of power might come to the thresholds of significance applied to various [[Law|laws]] and processes of the society. It is crucial to form resilient [[Government|governance]] systems which can define and update these thresholds as per the need of the civilization. Thresholds that are open to debate can be put to the [[Government|liquid democracy]] process, and the rest can be varied slightly within the limits of freedom to see what the measured outcomes say, as in the last point.
* Adapting a threshold is experimental design: gauge its [[freedom]] range, beyond which it is easily harmful; choose the outcomes it is meant to improve; perturb it within the range, observe, and shift it to the best performing value.
* Debate is the escape from that range. A proposal to move a threshold beyond its harm zone goes to a vote as a test case, and domain experts decide whether it is worth trying and at what scale: a share of the population or domains, for a limited time. The scale is itself a governed threshold. Whatever it is, the trial must show gains greater than harms and no irreparable [[harm]] to be adopted; a success may redraw the threshold's harm boundaries.
* A threshold is also the unit in which an [[Measurement#Assessment|assessed]] change is expressed: the difference an action makes is scaled by the threshold of significance of its context, so what counts as large or small depends on where it is measured.
* The parameters of the [[Benefuture goals#Mathematical approach|synergy operator]] used in the value calculation are thresholds in this sense. They are chosen, understood and tuned.
* Wherever possible, controlled [[Diversity|variance]] should be introduced in the value of thresholds and the outcomes carefully [[Measurement|measured]] to provide natural evolution towards upgraded effectiveness.

==Examples==
* A threshold of the amounts, durations and levels of [[harm]] that is permissible for a choice given the levels and amounts of [[understanding]] or [[happiness]] gained for it over time.
* The minimum amount of happiness generated by an activity for it to be recommended to [[sophont]]s by an [[administration]].
* A literal threshold, a line drawn in a water reservoir above which the water level is considered sufficient for use and not requiring refilling.
