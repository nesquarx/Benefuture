Input is the process of a [[thing]] absorbing information, energy, or matter from another [[thing]] in [[reality]] surrounding them. Usually resulting in a change in the [[thing]] taking the input.

==Aspects==
* An input, if it does not bring any change to the thing, may not be considered an input.
* A [[being]] often tends to [[interact]] with the world to have control on the inputs it takes.
* Inputs can be processed to extract further types of information.
* Inputs may be taken with or without control of the act of taking or the change it causes.

==Examples==
* Gaining information through any means.
* A raindrop hitting an animal's skin.
* Hearing (absorbing information as pressure waves), seeing (absorbing photons), touching (absorbing information from kinetic displacement of the boundaries of a [[body]]) or any other sensory input.
* Reading (seeing photons and processing their patterns as text).
* To be physically moved by something.
* A change in temperature or pressure in the environment causing a being to react.
* Colliding with another object and taking damage.
