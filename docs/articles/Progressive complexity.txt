Progressive complexity strategies ensure that [[System Design|systems]] can operate across a wide range of conditions and capabilities, from the simple to the complex. It involves employing strategies that allow systems to adapt, scale, and gracefully degrade, while continuing to serve the core [[goals]].

Progressive complexity is an approach to system design that embraces the inherent [[diversity]] and variability of [[reality]]. It posits that systems should be built to function effectively across a spectrum of conditions, technologies, and user capabilities. This is achieved by employing a series of "progressive" strategies and plans that allow the system to adapt its functionality and interface according to the available resources and the specific context. The core idea is to maximize the system's utility and resilience by ensuring it can always provide some level of service, even in degraded or constrained environments, while also being able to leverage advanced capabilities when available.

=== Aspects === 
* '''Progressive Fallback:''' Systems should be designed to maintain core functionality even as sophisticated technologies or resources become increasingly unavailable. This ensures continued operation, albeit at a reduced capacity, under adverse conditions. Any lack or corruption of capability, process, data or inputs falls back to simpler heuristics and toy models, ruling and acting in practice.
* '''Progressive Disclosure:''' Systems should reveal their functionality in layers, starting with a minimal set of touchpoints for basic operation and expanding to offer more complex [[Interaction|interactions]] as needed or as the user's proficiency increases.
* '''Progressive Abstraction:''' Systems should be built with multiple layers of abstraction, allowing users to interact with them at a level of detail appropriate to their needs and [[Understanding]], while hiding unnecessary complexity but not preventing access to it.
* '''Progressive Enhancement:''' Systems should begin with a solid foundation of core functionality that works in even the most basic environments, and then progressively enhance the user experience and add features as more advanced technologies become available. Any enhancement is consumed by researching and implementing means to use it better, keeping room for the [[Diversity|diversity–novelty balance]].
* '''Progressive Control:''' Systems should offer a range of control mechanisms, from simple, direct manipulation to more complex, automated, or AI-driven interactions, allowing users to choose the level of detail that suits their needs and abilities.
* '''Progressive Operation:''' Systems should be designed to operate across a spectrum of operational modes, from fully manual to fully autonomous, adapting to different contexts and user preferences.
* '''Progressive Change:''' Systems should be designed with feedback loops to evolve and adapt over time, accommodating new technologies, user needs, and environmental changes, without disrupting core functionality.

=== Examples ===
* '''[[Technology Network]] or [[Social networks]]:''' In a Benefuture technology network, while easily implementable via conventional networks, enhanced communication may occur via advanced BCI and AI-supported systems under optimal conditions. If those systems fail, the network can progressively fall back to the regular internet, then telephony, telegraphy, hand-delivered documents, verbal communication, and even basic gestures or attempts at communication with alien species. The network's core function of maximizing [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]] is maintained throughout these transitions.

* '''Manufacturing:''' A manufacturing method can be designed to operate at various levels of automation. Under ideal conditions, it might use fully automated robotic systems and state of the art technologies. If those systems fail, production can shift to semi-automated systems with human oversight. In a complete failure of automated systems, the method can still operate with manual labor, with low-tech tools, albeit at a reduced production rate. This ensures continued production, even under degraded conditions.

* '''[[Government]]:''' Benefuture's governance model uses expertise and impact-weighted direct democracy.  Sophonts can directly vote on policies, with their votes weighted by their demonstrated expertise and the degree to which they are affected. This allows for a progressive system where, for instance, a simple majority can enact basic laws, but more complex or impactful decisions require broader consensus and input from those most knowledgeable and affected. The government should continue to work under catastrophies where communication becomes fraught and needs to go into lowest levels of technology.

* '''[[Economy]] and [[Benefuture currency]]:''' The Benefuture economy employs a multi-currency system where different currencies compete based on their ability to generate societal [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]].  A new currency might start with a simple model, offering basic incentives. As its accuracy and effectiveness are proven, it gains wider acceptance and can incorporate more complex mechanisms.  This progressive adoption allows the economy to adapt and evolve. It also allows interactions at different levels of technology and complexity.

* '''[[Philosophy|Progressive Utilitarianism]]:''' In Benefuture, ethical decision-making follows a progressive utilitarian approach. When possible and feasible, each action or policy is scrutinized at a micro level (act utilitarianism) to precisely determine its impact on [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]]. When such granular analysis is not feasible due to time or complexity constraints, decision-making falls back to known rules and principles that have historically promoted these values (rule utilitarianism). This ensures both ethical precision and practical efficiency in governance and individual conduct.

* '''[[Education]]:''' The Benefuture education system emphasizes progressive enhancement.  Learners begin by acquiring fundamental meta-learning skills and critical thinking abilities.  As they progress, they gain access to more specialized knowledge and advanced tools, including ethically vetted cognitive enhancement technologies, allowing them to continually expand their capacity for [[Understanding]].

=== Discussion ===
Progressive complexity offers a robust and adaptable, holographic and fractal approach to [[system design]], aligning well with the [[Benefuture]] [[goals]] of maximizing [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]]. By ensuring that systems can function across a [[Diversity|diverse]] range of conditions and capabilities, it promotes resilience in the face of uncertainty, inclusivity for users with diverse needs and abilities, and continuous improvement through the incorporation of new technologies and knowledge. This approach acknowledges the dynamic and evolving nature of [[reality]], and seeks to create systems that can adapt and thrive in a constantly changing world. It also prevents loss of knowledge of ways to advance and provides optimal rebuilding plans given any catastrophic degradation in technology. It also means an extra expenditure in maintaining the knowledge and infrastructure of the graceful fallback strategies in a system, but it is in line with the goals to provide an unbroken memory and avoid negative black swan scenarios, which catastrophes often are.
