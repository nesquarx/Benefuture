[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
An entity is a [[thing]] which can perform [[interaction]]s and exhibits any amount of [[sapience]].

==Aspects==
* Entities with [[sentience]] can be a [[being]], a [[person]], or a [[benesophont]], but by default a non-sentient entity does not exhibit [[sentience]].
* Entities are capable of reaction. Hence entities require consideration while interacting with than non-entity [[Thing|things]].

==Examples==
* Most dynamic and reactive systems can be treated as entities.
* A machine with a particular purpose is an entity.
* All [[being]]s are entities.
* A collective group of beings operating under certain regulations can behave as a non-sentient entity, such as a corporation. In that case, the beings comprising it are treated as beings, but not the entity.

==Discussion==
===Minimal agency and corporate actors===
In the philosophy of information and artificial intelligence, Floridi and Sanders propose that agency should be defined at a clear ''level of abstraction'' by three criteria: interactivity (responding to stimuli from an environment), autonomy (changing state without direct external compulsion), and adaptability—deliberately separating agency from consciousness or moral personhood.<ref>Floridi, L., & Sanders, J. W. (2004). [https://doi.org/10.1023/B:MIND.0000035461.63578.9d On the Morality of Artificial Agents]. ''Minds and Machines'', 14(3), 349–379.</ref> List and Pettit extend this structural view to collective bodies, showing that corporations, committees, and states qualify as genuine institutional agents when they aggregate inputs into coherent outputs over time.<ref>List, C., & Pettit, P. (2011). [https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199591565.001.0001 ''Group Agency: The Possibility, Design, and Status of Corporate Agents'']. Oxford University Press.</ref>

Benefuture's category of ''entity'' captures this minimal interactive layer: any [[thing]] that takes [[input]]s and produces [[output]]s—whether an atom, a software process, a biological organism, or a corporation—is an entity. Because non-sentient entities cannot experience [[harm]], they have no intrinsic moral standing of their own; instead, control and [[Law|liability]] propagate through the interaction graph from non-sentient entities and tools back to the [[person]]s and [[benesophont]]s who direct them.

===In simulation===
* When an agent creates or controls a subordinate entity—such as a company or an automated tool—that causes downstream harm, propagating liability along the control graph assigns responsibility to the controlling agent rather than leaving it trapped behind a judgement-proof shell ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/docs/CLAIMS.md the claims record]).
