[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
An entity is a [[thing]] which can perform [[interaction]]s and exhibits any amount of [[sapience]].

==Aspects==
* Entities with [[sentience]] can be a [[being]], a [[person]], or a [[benesophont]], but by default a non-sentient entity does not exhibit [[sentience]].
* Entities are capable of reaction. Hence entities require consideration while interacting with than non-entity [[Thing|things]].

==Examples==
* Most dynamic and reactive systems can be treated as entities.
* A machine with a particular purpose is an entity.
* All [[being]]s are entities.
* A collective group of beings operating under certain regulations can behave as a non-sentient entity, such as a corporation. In that case, the beings comprising it are treated as beings, but not the entity.
