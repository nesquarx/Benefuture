A crime occurs when a set of events which can be traced to be triggered by informed volition, or informed inaction against a [[law]]'s mandate or a [[contract]], of one [[entity]] or more (criminal/s) - causes [[harm]] to one or more [[being]]s (victim/s).

==Aspects==
* The crime is of different levels depending upon the degree of harm to the victim, sometimes the degree of possible harm is also taken into consideration.
* The crime is also of different levels depending upon the level of [[understanding]] of the criminal of their actions - specifically their understanding of the possibility of harm to victims while triggering the actions.
* After a crime is discovered, proven and understood, [[justice]] in the form of [[Justice#Reparation|reparation]]s and [[Justice#Rehabilitation|rehabilitation]] for both criminals and victims is necessary.
* Often [[person]]s can be held to a higher standard as criminals than [[being]]s and [[entity|entities]]. Entities, however autonomous, can transfer their criminality to the [[person]]s responsible for the existence of the entity.
* Completed [[Education|infohazard education]] is evidence of being informed for harms within its scope.
* When harm is caused without informed volition or informed inaction against a mandate or contract by any entity, it is called an accident.
* A hazard one creates is direct harm: intent decides whether it is accident or crime, and repair happens either way.

==Formal reference==
The rules on this page as written in Benefuture's code ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture Benefuture on GitHub]): the proofs in Lean, and the Lean reference functions that the [[Simulation|simulation]] calls.
* Crime and accident are mutually exclusive; verified informed volition or an ignored warning is a crime, while uninformed or involuntary action is an accident ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/Benefuture/Subgoals/Justice.lean <code>crime_and_accident_mutually_exclusive</code>, <code>verified_informed_volition_is_crime</code>, <code>warning_ignored_constitutes_crime</code>, <code>uninformed_action_is_accident</code>, <code>involuntary_action_is_accident</code>]).
* Informed inaction under a duty from a contract or communicated law is a crime; inaction without a duty is not, and an uninformed breach is an accident ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/Benefuture/Subgoals/Justice.lean <code>informed_inaction_under_duty_is_crime</code>, <code>inaction_without_duty_is_not_crime</code>, <code>uninformed_breach_is_accident</code>]; [https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/BenefutureReference/Justice.lean <code>is_crime_ref</code>]).
* Liability scales with ontological capacity ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/Benefuture/Ontology/Law.lean <code>liability_hierarchy_order</code>, <code>ontological_liability_ordering</code>]).
