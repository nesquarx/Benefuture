[[category:Benefuture]] [[category:WorldBuilding]]
A crime occurs when a set of events which can be traced to be triggered by informed volition, or informed inaction against a [[law]]'s mandate or a [[contract]], of one [[entity]] or more (criminal/s) - causes [[harm]] to one or more [[being]]s (victim/s).

==Aspects==
* The crime is of different levels depending upon the degree of harm to the victim, sometimes the degree of possible harm is also taken into consideration.
* The crime is also of different levels depending upon the level of [[understanding]] of the criminal of their actions - specifically their understanding of the possibility of harm to victims while triggering the actions.
* After a crime is discovered, proven and understood, [[justice]] in the form of [[Justice#Reparation|reparation]]s and [[Justice#Rehabilitation|rehabilitation]] for both criminals and victims is necessary.
* Often [[person]]s can be held to a higher standard as criminals than [[being]]s and [[entity|entities]]. Entities, however autonomous, can transfer their criminality to the [[person]]s responsible for the existence of the entity.
* Completed [[Education|infohazard education]] is evidence of being informed for harms within its scope.
* When harm is caused without informed volition or informed inaction by any entity, it is called an accident.
* A hazard one creates is direct harm: intent decides whether it is accident or crime, and repair happens either way.
