[[Category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
'''Morality''' is the [[understanding]] that the chance of an [[interaction]] between a [[Being|being's]] [[Being#Self|self]] and [[reality]] making no [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal contribution]] to the self's [[outcome]]s is almost zero, given that the self's memory is among those outcomes. A one-shot game lies outside reality.

==Aspects==
* Morality is an understanding: it grows with understanding of the self's true extent, and it is read through [[output]]s, since beliefs and preferences are not directly accessible.
* Isolation is almost never real. Influence spreads as a probability; stricter measures of influence take longer to saturate, but strictness sets the lag, not the destination.
* The self extends over everything in reality whose harm would become harm to the being over the [[Benefuture goals#Time|long term]], recognised or not. The lifespan bounds recognition, not intent.
* Every game is shaped by its participants' memory and shapes the games they play next, so supposed one-shot games join one network.
* Any information leaking from a game is a causal contribution: who played, what happened, how it was structured, or the bare fact that it was considered. Anything known, simulated or speculated about its outcome has already left it.
* Memory is an outcome. A being that defects and profits knows it, and becomes the kind that defects; the chance that its isolations stay real shrinks with each repetition.
* A true one-shot game needs no memory going in and nothing leaving it: unperceivable, outside reality, and irrelevant to [[measurement]].

==Examples==
* '''An empty beach:''' littering unseen still enters memory, forms habit, and reaches the beach's later users.
* '''A deathbed fraud:''' [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] keeps attributing it to the fraudster's record and to their causal and ideological heirs.
* '''An unseen theft:''' the act is hidden, the gain is not; spending it is an output, and outputs are logged.
* '''Strangers splitting a reward:''' each brings a history into the game and carries a story out of it.
* '''A fork that defects and ends:''' its liability spills over to its sources.
* '''A psychopath:''' harms others without regard, recognising a self that stops at their own skin. The causal self still extends over those harmed; the defect is insufficient understanding of it, not a self that is truly smaller.
* '''A speculated game:''' a game imagined but never played still enters memory, and what was learned from imagining it shapes later games. Considering a game is already playing a part of it.

==Discussion==
===The self===
The self has two readings. The causal self is objective: everything whose harm would return to the being across the long-term window, including its history and records and much it never recognises. The recognised self is a cognitive artefact: a being may recognise anything as its self, its [[reputation]], the beings it loves, the things it identifies with, and values their lifespans, so its timeline stretches with theirs, even past its own. Recognition is not measured. Harm to anything is measured through the usual [[Benefuture goals#Harm|harm tiers]], so identifying with a thing turns no one's choices that leave it untouched into harm, and neither self gives any claim over its self-things beyond established relations: [[contract]]s, [[law]] and [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]]. The lifespan bounds recognition, not intent. Property is not inherited, but causal and ideological inheritance continue, as causal distribution demands: outcomes keep being attributed to a being's record, recounted from memory, and to those who carry its causal chains and ideas forward. Morality closes the gap between the two selves; a being whose recognised self is narrow, like the psychopath in the examples, is not smaller, only less understanding of its extent.

===Propagation===
Influence propagates as a probability rather than a realised spread; a single instance may never show the curve, and its shape appears only statistically. Influence through agents grows exponentially until it nears the bound set by physical influence, the light cone,<ref>Minkowski, H. (1909). [https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Raum_und_Zeit_(Minkowski) Raum und Zeit]. ''Physikalische Zeitschrift'', 10, 104–111.</ref> then flattens toward it. The curves form a family indexed by how strict the measure of influence is: pure causality is the loosest, a particular class of decisions being taken is stricter, and being known to people is stricter still. Sensitive dependence on initial conditions makes strictness matter less over time,<ref>Lorenz, E. N. (1963). [https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469(1963)020%3C0130:DNF%3E2.0.CO;2 Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow]. ''Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences'', 20(2), 130–141.</ref> so even the strictest measure keeps a chance of spreading within a cone that eventually runs parallel to the light cone, inside it, never reaching it because full saturation is too improbable. Saturation is not a uniform blur in which consequences stop mattering; it is the most consequential state, where consequences have reached their natural extent. Benefuture aims to shorten the gap to it. A contribution may be too small or too slow to estimate; that is a limit of estimation, not isolation, and Benefuture attempts to make causal propagation more transparent and tractable.

What follows from an influence depends not only on the light cone of the event that started it, but on the cones along the whole history in spacetime of the entity that decided, its worm,<ref>Sider, T. (2001). [https://openlibrary.org/isbn/019924443X ''Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence and Time'']. Oxford University Press.</ref> which can include regions outside the event's own cone. The [[Being#Self|extended self]] changes the shape: upstream a reverse cone, as surrounding influence converges toward the event (whether it also flares outward is open); downstream a trumpet, narrow at first, spreading slowly, then curving out to fill the parallel cone. The forward trumpet of any supposed one-shot game feeds the reverse cone of every later game its participants take part in, so propagation forms a closed network across games rather than separate trajectories. One-shot-ness can be narrowed by class, timing or specificity, but the event still depends on its memory cone and still emits its trumpet of effects; what remains would need a game played in an isolated universe, irrelevant outside it. Anonymity, terminal states and acts on the scale of extinction fall to this first: each still happens within reality, and each still leaves effects in it. The claim is not that every game is literally repeated, but that every game is shaped by earlier games and shapes later ones. Since knowing, simulating or speculating about a game already leaks from it, the one-shot game resembles the game in the meme: thinking about it loses it.

===Memory, not repetition===
Cooperation in repeated games depends on the shadow of the future,<ref>Axelrod, R., & Hamilton, W. D. (1981). [https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7466396 The Evolution of Cooperation]. ''Science'', 211(4489), 1390–1396.</ref> but the active ingredient is memory: recognising past opponents and responding to them consistently is enough for cooperation to emerge, without kinship, reputation or population structure.<ref>Morozov, A. V., & Feigel, A. (2026). [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2513282123 Emergence of cooperation due to opponent-specific responses in Prisoner's Dilemma]. ''Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences'', 123(21), e2513282123.</ref> Benefuture's claim is more minimal: any information leaking from a game is enough, and binding [[identity]] is one rich case of leakage.

Memory also works inside the defector. A being does not choose a single act but becomes the kind of being that acts so, and that disposition applies to every similar situation that follows. Character formed by habituation is Aristotle's;<ref>Aristotle. (c. 340 BCE). [http://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.html ''Nicomachean Ethics''], Book II.</ref> assessing dispositions rather than acts is Gauthier's constrained maximisation;<ref>Gauthier, D. (1986). [https://openlibrary.org/isbn/0198249926 ''Morals by Agreement'']. Oxford University Press.</ref> and deciding as if choosing one's decision procedure across all its instances is functional decision theory.<ref>Yudkowsky, E., & Soares, N. (2017). [https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05060 Functional Decision Theory: A New Theory of Instrumental Rationality]. ''arXiv'':1710.05060.</ref> If each defection is truly isolated with probability p, a policy of defecting survives n uses with probability p to the power n. In today's reality that may take many repetitions to fail; in Benefuture it fails far sooner, because its institutions are committed to predicting and measuring, exposure is retroactive through an immutable memory from which [[reputation]] is recounted, and the gain is as visible as the act. Plato's ring of invisibility asks whether anyone would stay just if guaranteed impunity;<ref>Plato. (c. 375 BCE). [http://classics.mit.edu/Plato/republic.html ''Republic''], Book II.</ref> impunity for the act is not impunity for the gain.

===Place among moral theories===
Decision theory refutes the one-shot framing; morality is understanding that refutation; [[measurement]], by making propagation visible, is what turns the one into the other. Like Hume's justice, the morality is completed by conventions that make it pay:<ref>Hume, D. (1739–40). [https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4705 ''A Treatise of Human Nature''], Book 3, Part 2.</ref> true of reality in the limit, and brought closer to saturation by institutions. Because the self extends beyond what a being recognises, morality grows with understanding, close to the Socratic view that no one does wrong willingly;<ref>Plato. (c. 380 BCE). [http://classics.mit.edu/Plato/protagoras.html ''Protagoras''].</ref> weakness of will, read through outputs, is a failure to predict one's own outputs, itself a gap in understanding. Parfit reached impartiality by weakening the self until others mattered as much;<ref>Parfit, D. (1984). [https://openlibrary.org/isbn/019824908X ''Reasons and Persons''], Part III. Oxford University Press.</ref> Benefuture reaches it by extending the self until others fall within it. Moral frameworks that cannot be mapped onto this view tend to rest on variables no output reveals.

Morality is not the source of value. Under optimistic nihilism the [[Benefuture goals|goal]] says what is valued, morality explains why a being that understands correctly complies, and [[law]] and [[enforcement]] cover the lag before saturation, during which a defection's return can be smaller than its gain.
