[[category:Benefuture]]
An outcome set of changes happening in [[reality]] as percieved by an [[entity]].

==Aspects==
* A change in reality is called an outcome when it is of some [[significance]] to an [[entity]].
* Outcomes are commonly anticipated positively or negatively, or planned for, and [[output]]s performed specifically to make that change happen or not happen.

==Examples==
* A being repeatedly [[input]]s sustenance to effect the outcome of their continued survival.
* An [[entity]] performs certain [[output]]s to bring forth the outcome of increased [[understanding]] obtained by certain [[person]]s.
* A [[civilization]] undertaking a complex geological landscaping to avoid the outcome of a natural disaster.
