[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
A thing is an identifiable pattern of information, rendered in energy, matter or as an expression inside another pattern of information.  

==Aspects==
* A thing can be a part of another thing and can contain other things.
* A thing can refer to a certain pattern that defines the commonality among a set of things. Such things are generally known as abstract things.
* Not every thing is part of [[reality]]. Then those things are abstract or simulated.

==Examples==
* An object in [[reality]] is a real thing.
* An idea or concept which can be described using real things is an abstract thing.
* An [[entity]], [[being]] or [[person]] is a thing.
* Any piece of information is a thing.
* The [[Identity]] of a thing is tied to its pattern and location in reality.
