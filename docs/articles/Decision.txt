A decision or choice is an [[output]] made by an [[entity]] intending to achieve a certain outcome. 
Generally it is a process of:
# [[Understanding]] certain aspects of [[reality]] via [[input]]s.
# Speculating possible outputs and their effects on reality.
# Executing the output which is speculated to affect reality in line with the [[goals]] of the entity.

==Aspects==
* A choice is not made until its declared or acted upon.
* Decisions can change in the midst of execution, but unless the change is a reaction to a change of inputs, it can denote an error in previous speculation.

==Examples==
* A [[person]] takes their own capabilities and the current state of the market as inputs, then creates goals with how they envision the future to be and their interests, speculates on the future needs of the market, then decides to build their career in a way which meet both the needs of the markets and their interests and leads to realizing their goals.
