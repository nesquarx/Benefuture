[[category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
An output is an action, when a [[thing]] causes changes in [[reality]]. Usually via changing the [[body]], generally causing movement, communication, change in appearance, or excretion.

==Aspects==
* For a [[being]], all output may not come from specific intent. Usually the existence of beings often causes outputs as a result of the process of their existence.
* Output with the specific intent to convey information is called [[communication]].
* Some processes can be mixed as they can [[input]] as well as output.


==Examples==
* A [[being]] changing their extremities to create patterns of light or sound to convey information.
* A [[thing]] changing its position to change the position of things around it.
* A [[being]] excreting as part of its process of existence.
