The [[Technology Network]] is the decentralized, transparent, and evolving computational infrastructure underpinning the [[Life in benefuture|lived experiences]] in the [[Benefuture]] system. It facilitates the [[Measurement|measurement]] of [[Understanding]] (U) and [[Happiness]] (H), the operation of [[Benefuture currency|competing currencies]], and the functioning of [[Government|weighted direct democracy]]. It is deeply enmeshed with [[Social networks|social networks]], leveraging their data and providing tools for enhanced [[communication]], [[education]] and collaboration.

==Aspects==

* '''Decentralized and Distributed:''' The network is not controlled by any single entity. It's composed of numerous independent nodes, distributed globally, ensuring resilience and preventing censorship.
* '''Transparent and Open Source:''' The core algorithms and (anonymized) data used by the network are publicly accessible and auditable. This promotes trust and allows for continuous improvement.
* '''Multiple Competing Components:''' The network is not monolithic. It hosts a dynamic ecosystem of:
** Multiple protocols and clusters competing for effectiveness but cooperating for communication.
** Competing [[Measurement|metric models]] for assessing U/H.
** Competing [[Benefuture currency|currency networks]], each based on one or more metric models.
** Competing knowledge graphs for categorizing actions, transactions, and concepts.
** Competing algorithms for various tasks (e.g., identity mapping, fraud detection, bias mitigation, resource allocation).
* '''Heterogeneous Clusters:''' Clusters differ in the technology they use, so a failure in one is unlikely to be shared by the others. What they do share is a minimal core of data-sharing and security protocols, which is the one place a single failure could spread to all of them, so it is kept minimal, open source and under standing audit.
* '''Transaction Log:''' Every action is recorded as a transaction and every record triggers [[Measurement|measurement]]. This is the [[System Design#Behavioral Transaction Substrate|behavioral transaction substrate]] of the network, kept immutable, and the source of the [[Benefuture goals#Vision|Unbroken Memory]].
* '''Enmeshment with [[Social networks|Social Networks]]:'''
** '''Identity binding:''' Competing networks can propose methods, the competition is exactly like [[Measurement|metric models]], the most effective methods are used together to corroborate and bind [[Identity|Identities]] 1:1 to the participant [[entities]] of the networks. This binding is graded by confidence from the networks, inter-cluster correlations, and methods, (from the vaguest already logged in account, to the deepest biological fingerprinting or digital source or temporally chunked memory hashing, even validating recorded continuity of the tracked [[body]] of the ID'd entity). And [[Benefuture currency#Transaction Mechanics|transaction]] types require [[Threshold of significance|thresholded]] grades of cross-cluster binding confidence (with methods from at least two clusters) as well as the transaction records store the methods and confidences of the bound IDs for [[Justice|forensic]] purposes. Transactions are flagged for [[Justice|adjudication]] if more than one bound entity per identity or vice versa is discovered above threshold.
** '''Data Source:''' Social networks provide a rich source of (anonymized and aggregated) data for U/H measurement.
** '''Layered privacy:''' Privacy is implicit in all generic data queries, anonymized data reveals no specifics yet provides robust research requirements with iteration limits to maintain anonymity, while specific queries and sharing of PII of individuals is strictly transparent and two way, all queries are logged, [[Social networks|info-hazard gated]] with [[Education|educational]] requirements, viewers of PII data have to give away reciprocal access, who would still have to clear the educational requirements to access them both ways. Third party sharing is gated, backtracked and exposed the same way.
** '''Communication Platform:''' The Technology Network provides tools and infrastructure for enhanced communication and collaboration within social networks, including:
*** [[Truth]] level verification tools.
*** [[Untruth|Misinformation]] detection and flagging.
*** Source tracking of information.
*** Mapping contention for statements under debate.
*** Infohazard detection and gating, [[education]] prerequisite determination and path provisioning. Gating is only legitimate where a path to access through education is provided, and is held to strict scrutiny as a limit on the freedom to know the [[Truth|truth]].
*** Privacy tracking and reciprocal privacy enablement.
*** Facilitation of deliberative [[Government|democracy]] (weighted voting, expert consultation).
*** Secure and transparent [[Benefuture currency|transaction]] processing.
* '''Continuous Learning and Adaptation:''' The network constantly learns and adapts, driven by real-world data and the competition between metric models and currencies.
* '''Sophont Oversight:''' While largely automated, the system incorporates mechanisms for human (sophont) auditing, oversight and intervention, particularly in cases of disputes, ethical dilemmas, or unexpected outcomes.
* As a key system of  [[benefuture]] [[civilization]], [[Anti-Corruption|anti-corruption]] measures, [[Adversarial adaptation]] and [[Progressive complexity|Progressive strategies]] also need to be applied in its [[System Design|system design]].

==Initial Feasible Technologies==

The initial implementation of the [[Technology Network]] can leverage existing, proven technologies:

* '''Blockchain Technology:''' A distributed, immutable ledger for recording all transactions and data related to U/H measurement. This ensures transparency and prevents tampering. Multiple blockchains may exist, corresponding to different currencies, metrics or transactional ledgers.
* '''Smart Contracts:''' Self-executing contracts that automate the rules and processes of the [[Benefuture currency]] system, including front-loaded and back-loaded payments, probabilistic escrow, and novelty bonuses. Even some basic [[Law|laws]] can be embedded into transactions.
* '''Decentralized Knowledge Graphs:''' Networks of interconnected data that represent knowledge about the world, used for categorizing actions, transactions, and concepts. Multiple, competing knowledge graphs would exist, providing diverse perspectives.
* '''Federated Learning:''' A machine learning technique that allows models to be trained on decentralized data without directly accessing the data itself, preserving privacy.
* '''Differential Privacy:''' Techniques for adding noise to data to protect individual privacy while still allowing for aggregate analysis with query cutoffs or data diffusion over composition attack [[Threshold of significance|thresholds]].
* '''Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC):''' Cryptographic protocols that allow multiple parties to jointly compute a function on their private inputs without revealing those inputs to each other.
* '''Statistical Analysis Tools:''' A range of statistical methods for analyzing data, identifying correlations, and assessing the performance of metric models (e.g., regression analysis, Bayesian methods, time series analysis).
* '''Existing Social Network APIs:''' (With user consent and strong privacy protections) to gather data on communication patterns, social connections, and sentiment.

==Potential Future Technologies==

As technology advances, the [[Technology Network]] could incorporate:

* '''Large Concept Models (LCMs):''' Advanced AI models, analogous to Large Language Models (LLMs), but trained on broader, more structured knowledge representations (including knowledge graphs and causal models). Multiple, competing LCMs could be used to categorize transactions, assess U/H impact, and detect anomalies. These would be designed to be explainable and transparent.
* '''Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs):''' DAOs could manage individual currency networks, allowing for more flexible and adaptive governance.
* '''Advanced Cryptographic Techniques:''' Zero-knowledge proofs, homomorphic encryption, and other advanced cryptographic techniques could further enhance privacy and security.
* '''Neuromorphic Computing:''' Brain-inspired computing architectures could provide the computational power needed for more sophisticated U/H modeling.
* '''Quantum Computing:''' (Further in the future) Quantum computing could revolutionize certain aspects of the network, such as optimization and cryptography.
* '''Advanced Biosensors:''' (With strict ethical guidelines) Non-invasive, real-time monitoring of physiological and neurological data could provide richer, more objective measures of U/H.
* '''Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs):''' (Long-term, highly speculative, and ethically complex) BCIs could potentially allow for direct measurement of cognitive and emotional states, but may also raise significant privacy and autonomy concerns.

==Discussion==

The [[Technology Network]] is the engine that drives the [[Benefuture]] system. It is not a single, monolithic entity, but rather a dynamic, evolving ecosystem of competing and collaborating components. This decentralized, transparent, and data-driven approach is crucial for ensuring the system's robustness, adaptability, and ethical integrity. The deep enmeshment with [[Social networks|social networks]] allows for both leveraging the vast amounts of data generated by human interaction and providing tools to enhance the quality and truthfulness of that interaction. The continuous competition between metric models, currencies, and algorithms, combined with human oversight, is designed to prevent the system from stagnating or being captured by any single interest group. The ongoing development and integration of new technologies will be essential for realizing the full potential of the [[Benefuture]] vision.
