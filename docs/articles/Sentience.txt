Sentience is the quality of an [[entity]] exhibiting [[preference]]s and a threshold of consciousness which perceives or attempts to modify [[reality]] in a self-aware fashion with intent. A sentient entity is called a [[Being]]
==Aspects==
* The definition of consciousness is still evolving<ref>The quest to detect consciousness — in all its possible forms. (2025). ''Nature'', ''643'', 1172–1174. https://doi.org/10.1038/d41586-025-02349-5</ref>, but we assume a panpsychic<ref>Goff, Philip, William Seager, and Sean Allen-Hermanson, "Panpsychism", ''The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy'' (Summer 2022 Edition), Edward N. Zalta (ed.), https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2022/entries/panpsychism/</ref> nature of it where it is a sliding scale of different levels ranging from rigid programmed interactions to complete self awareness.
* We assume the threshold of consciousness required to be deemed a sentient being to be at a level where it [[understanding|understands]] itself sufficiently to engage in [[civilization]]. This is soft threshold, there are more detailed [[person#Sentience_thresholds|thresholds]] for personhood.
* Sentient entities can be assumed to hold a level of agency and intent and may be held responsible for their [[interaction]]s.
