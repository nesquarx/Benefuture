[[Category:Benefuture]] [[Category:Definitions]]
A [[being]] is an [[entity]] which exhibits [[sentience]]. Beings are the most rudimentary denomination of sentient entities which a [[civilization]] might treat as possessing [[Person#Sentience thresholds|agency]] and capable of experiencing [[harm]] in varying extents.

==Aspects==
* A being usually exhibits preferences in its [[input]]s, where it produces [[output]]s so as to maximise certain inputs and minimise certain other inputs.
* A being usually exhibits agency over itself, its inputs and outputs exhibit a pattern of self governance where patterns of intent can be observed which then culminate to the intended outcome.
* The levels of interaction, sentience and sapience can vary widely, the [[threshold of significance]] to be recognised as a being is low. At a high enough threshold of sentience and sapience, a being can be called a [[person]].

==Boundaries==
===Lifespan===
The time from when a [[thing]] first exhibits [[sentience]], [[sapience]] and [[interaction]] - till its pattern changes such that it cannot be recognised as, or reverted back to, that particular being - is called its '''lifespan'''. The pattern that exists before it begins and after it ends is not the being. The lifespan bounds when harm can be experienced, not how far the self reaches or what the being intends. When it ends, property is not inherited, but causal and ideological inheritance continue: [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] keeps attributing outcomes to the being's record and to those who carry its causal chains forward, and its ideas persist through [[culture]].

===Body===
The dimensional space occupied in [[reality]] by the being and its contents, generally bounded by the extremities from where it can directly sense inputs and control outputs, such that harm to any of the contents causes immediate harm to the being, usually heuristically bounded from where its pattern is commonly recognisable as a type of being, is called its '''body'''.

[[Entity|Entities]] can also have a body which is more distributed or discorporate or defined via boundaries of information systems. There are usually corresponding, sometimes shared physical systems which holds and transmits the informations, including other entities and beings, all can be said to be recognisable as the body of that type of entity.

==== Identity ====
Identity is rooted in the uniqueness of the pattern of the body and can be disambiguated by its location and causal history. A pattern that is not unique in [[Reality]] is not an identity but a [[domain]] pattern. If identity forks, (or merges), associated systemic [[power]] such as [[currency]] and [[reputation]] up to the moment are divided per [[domain]] (or summed) and new disambiguated identities (or identity) are established as needed. The base [[Government|vote]] is part of the power a fork divides; a copy earns its own only as its novelty separates it into a distinct identity. Human birth is a merge of two forks, partialised by memory loss: by the time personhood suffices for a relevant vote, enough novel identity and reputation has accrued for a fresh base vote. A base vote follows novelty and personhood, not identity count. [[Entities]] and [[Thing|things]] can have identity too based on their patterns. Even if a forked identity reaches end of lifespan, liability spills over to sources via [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|Causal distribution]]. [[Diversity|Novelty]] is only as much as the identities differ at any given time, and a high amount of forked beings with minimal difference can be considered a monoculture risk and a possible resource accounting sink. A high amount of forked entities or things are only valid for their replicated [[Benefuture goals|utility]] and should be controlled as needed.

===Self===
A more indistinct boundary, is the boundary starting from the least part of it beyond which the thing cannot function as a being, stretching beyond the body to all the parts which the being recognises as parts of the same being, can be called its '''self'''. A being's sense of self can further extend fuzzily and arbitrarily over other things which the being associates with itself, and such any interaction to and from those things can seem like an interaction with its self, where harm to those can trickle down as harm to the being over time. The being's memory is part of its self, so remembering an interaction is itself an outcome of that interaction for the self (see [[Morality|morality]]).

The self has two readings. The '''causal self''' is everything in [[reality]] whose harm would become harm to the being across the [[Benefuture goals#Time|long term]], from the deepest record to the furthest prediction. It includes the being's history and records, and parts of reality the being does not recognise. Its extent is not chosen, and a being's understanding of it can fall short of it; closing that gap is what [[Morality|morality]] is. The '''recognised self''' is the cognitive artefact described above: a being may recognise anything as its self, and it values the lifespans of what it recognises (its [[reputation]], the beings it loves, the things it identifies with) even beyond its own lifespan. Recognition is not itself measured. When harm to anything is [[measurement|measured]], the usual [[Benefuture goals#Harm|harm tiers]] apply, so identifying with a thing does not turn others' choices that leave it untouched into harm. Neither reading of the self confers any claim over its self-things beyond established relations: including a thing in one's self gives no ownership, control or standing over it that [[contract]]s, [[law]] and [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] do not already give.

==Discussion==
===Autopoiesis, 4D spacetime worms, and branching identity===
Biological and systems accounts of agency ground ''being'' in self-maintaining organization—what Maturana and Varela termed ''autopoiesis'', where an organism continuously regenerates the boundary and processes that sustain it against entropic decay.<ref>Maturana, H. R., & Varela, F. J. (1980). [https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-009-8947-4 ''Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living'']. D. Reidel Publishing Company.</ref> Across time, Benefuture's definition of identity as the continuous spacetime pattern of existence from beginning to end directly parallels the ''four-dimensionalist'' (perdurance) view in metaphysics—developed by Quine, Lewis, and Sider—in which a persisting being is a four-dimensional ''spacetime worm'' composed of causally and psychologically connected temporal stages, rather than a static object that must remain unchanged from moment to moment.<ref>Lewis, D. K. (1983). ''Survival and Identity''. In [https://doi.org/10.1093/0195032047.003.0005 ''Philosophical Papers''] (Vol. I, pp. 55–77). Oxford University Press.</ref><ref>Sider, T. (2001). [https://doi.org/10.1093/019924443X.001.0001 ''Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence and Time'']. Clarendon Press.</ref>

Viewing identity as a 4D spacetime trajectory also resolves the puzzles of psychological continuity<ref>Locke, J. (1689). [https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10615 ''An Essay Concerning Human Understanding''] (Book II, Chapter XXVII). Thomas Basset.</ref> when minds branch (fission) or combine (fusion). As Parfit and Lewis showed, when a trajectory splits into two branches or two trajectories merge, they share an overlapping 4D segment of history while diverging thereafter ("Relation R").<ref>Parfit, D. (1984). [https://doi.org/10.1093/019824908X.001.0001 ''Reasons and Persons''] (Part Three). Clarendon Press.</ref> Benefuture operationalizes branching and merging directly on that trajectory: when a being forks, its [[Benefuture currency|currency]] and reputation split across the branches without minting new social capital and with zero initial [[Diversity|novelty]] for the duplicate; when beings merge, reputation is conserved and distinct novelty accumulates.

===The extended self===
Clark and Chalmers famously argued for the ''extended mind'': when an external tool, notebook, or environment is reliably coupled to an agent's cognitive loop, it functions as part of that agent's mind rather than mere external scaffolding.<ref>Clark, A., & Chalmers, D. (1998). [https://doi.org/10.1093/analys/58.1.7 The Extended Mind]. ''Analysis'', 58(1), 7–19.</ref> Benefuture generalizes this from cognition to axiology and [[morality]]: a being's ''extended self'' comprises every [[thing]] and [[being]] with which it interacts, weighted by interaction strength, and grows as [[understanding]] reveals deeper interdependence with family, community, and ecology. Because predicted harm to the extended self registers in the being's own evaluation even after the being's individual lifespan ends, an expanded self naturally internalizes post-mortem and intergenerational consequences without requiring external coercion.

===In simulation===
* Removing a being cannot raise the population floor: the floor is taken over the beings present at decision time, so removing the worst-off for gains to others is never chosen; in tragic choices, the trolley switch is chosen except when the one holds the peak of understanding ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/docs/CLAIMS.md the claims record]).
* Forced additions and accessions are met with uplift while protecting existing beings over their long-term window, and births are escrowed against predicted shortfall.
* When a being's extended self includes its community, predicted harm to that community after the being's own lifespan ends still detracts from the being's choice-time evaluation, eliminating end-of-horizon short-termism even before [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] is applied ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/docs/CLAIMS.md the claims record]).
