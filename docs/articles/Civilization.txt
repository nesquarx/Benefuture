[[Category:Benefuture]] [[category:Definitions]] [[category:WorldBuilding]]
A civilization or society is a system of [[law]]s of [[interaction]]s where a collective of [[person]]s can collaborate to maximise the realisation of their common [[goals]] to a greater extent than they can individually. When the [[goals]] are the [[benefuture]] goals then the members of the society are called [[benesophont]]s.

==Aspects==
* The system of a civilization or a society consists of common sets of rules for multiple kinds of [[interaction]]s between [[person]]s, [[entity|entities]] or [[being]]s, sometimes organically formed, sometimes engineered to serve its goals, these rules are usually codified as their [[law]]s.

* The infrastructure for creation, modification, implementation, and enforcement of these rules is maintained by an [[entity]] called the [[administration]] which is instantiated, moderated, operated and ratified by the [[sophont]]s who are members of that society.

==Examples==
* A [[benefuture]] society basically is a group of [[sophont]]s who strive to strive to gain the most [[understanding]] and then [[happiness]] through their present and future, creating an [[administration]] which enables this maximisation and results in the [[benesophont]]s living and growing by the [[law]]s it maintains.

==Discussion==
===In simulation===
* Composed multi-decade and century simulations with rare catastrophes and breakthroughs test civilisational continuity and stability across 5,000 to 20,000 steps; unassigned catastrophes repaired through the commons recover in one step, and breakthroughs raise floor growth by a third ([https://github.com/nesquarx/Benefuture/blob/main/docs/CLAIMS.md the claims record]).
