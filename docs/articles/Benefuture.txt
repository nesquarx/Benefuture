[[Category:Benefuture]] [[category:Definitions]] [[category:WorldBuilding]]

[[Benefuture]] is a [[philosophy]] of [[civilization]] which aims to maximise universal [[understanding]], then [[happiness]], and their synergistic effects over time - as the ultimate utility of [[Life in benefuture|life]], in Sanskrit, somewhat akin to Gyananandam (Jnanandam). It is a [[System Design|system]] extrapolating from those core [[goals]] for [[sentience|sentient]] [[sapience|sapient]] [[sophont]]s to exist in [[reality]]. It can be [[Implementation prototype|implemented]] in modern society via gradual non-violent change by [[Measurement|measuring]] and [[Benefuture currency|incentivizing]] [[good]].

It is a consequentialist philosophy with roots in Utilitarianism but instead of just pleasure or happiness as the ultimate goal it posits understanding as the ultimate goal, followed by happiness, since understanding creates and compounds meta-capability.

The philosophy does not claim any natural or preordained purpose. It is optimistically Nihilistic and imposes the [[Benefuture goals|utility function]] of universal [[understanding]] and [[happiness]] with a weight towards [[diversity|novelty]] as a fulfilling outcome and driving force of [[benesophont|sophonce]] and [[civilization]] via [[government]], [[economy]], [[currency]], [[administration]], [[Law|laws]], [[justice]], [[culture]], [[Technology Network|technology]] etc.

==Arguments==

* [[Understanding]] is a unique capacity of [[sapience|sapient]] [[entities]] in [[reality|the universe]] which [[Philosophy#Empirical Support|compounds]] and creates a substrate for comprehending anything.
* [[Sentience|Sentient]] [[Being|beings]] can self-reflect and change [[reality]] with intent and [[preference]].
* Hence, the emergence of [[Sapience|sapient]] life is poetically called the universe's attempt to understand itself. <ref>Cox, Brian. (Presenter). (2011). ''Wonders of the Universe'' [Television series]. BBC.</ref> <ref>Sagan, C. (1980). ''Cosmos''. Random House.</ref>
* In humanity's limited scope, we have seen greater [[understanding]] of [[reality]] to improve the overall quantity and quality of [[sentience]], [[sapience]] and also [[happiness]] of [[Sophont|sophonts]].
* Hence, (sophonts) [[understanding]] ([[reality]]) increases the amount of [[understanding]] and [[happiness]] in the universe.
* Understanding and happiness have a [[Benefuture goals#Mathematical approach|synergistic]] effect and pursuing that synergy will further enhance both over time.
* With that goal in mind, let us propose a [[Philosophy|cognitive prior]] to Utilitarianism by prioritising understanding over happiness because understanding generalizes and increases happiness, but not let go of happiness as generated by things apart from understanding. Hence the dual [[goals]] of understanding and then happiness. 
* Then we frame a [[philosophy]] of [[civilization]] based upon the [[goals]].
* And that philosophy provides us a benchmark to build a civilization of [[Benesophont|sophonts]] in collaboration with [[entities]], [[being]]s, and other [[person]]s we may come across who may aspire to follow or not follow the same goals.
* Realizing that any system of civilization, especially our current [[reality]], suffers from critical failure modes, we attempt to build [[System Design|antifragility]] into the processes of such a civilization, so that even if we can't avoid them, we can learn to identify them at n-meta levels and best mitigate them.
* Aiming for a [[Benefuture goals#Vision|vision]] of an ever flourishing, ever learning, [[Happiness|eudaimonic]] civilization which can aim to utilize, assimilate, understand and increase the scope of known [[reality]].

==Critical Evaluation==
Benefuture is a philosophy woven from multiple ideas, some proven, some with merit but not proven, it is aware of the obvious criticisms and pitfalls of such broad ideologies and tries to minimize, mitigate and grow through critical interrogation. The key aspect to consider is that it is a not a solution to the problems of civilization, rather a rough process outline of how we might discover those problems, find the solutions, implement them, and keep improving on the process. All the while learning from our mistakes. We will need to fill in the gaps in the outline with updated knowledge from [[reality]].

Current reality consists of fragmentary, misaligned, captured approximations of principles that evolved historically without comprehensive framework, are undermined by perverse incentives and power concentration, but which reveal humanity's persistent desire for systems serving understanding and happiness. Reform movements are attempting to complete this fragmentary project but lack systematic integration. BeneFuture attempts to refine and build the comprehensive framework and correction mechanisms to be able to converge on what we've been clumsily attempting piecemeal for centuries.

Critical analysis of common concerns about Benefuture must be considered beyond systemic critique in a vacuum, they must be held in comparison to similar concerns in today's reality and whether the interwoven autocorrecting systems of Benefuture can show a path to handling those concerns better than how they are handled today. Unknown unknowns have existed and will continue to exist, can we better know and handle them through the current or the proposed models? Common critiques have been [[Critique addressal|addressed]], beyond that, [[Adversarial adaptation|adversarial]] critique is eagerly welcome.
