The '''[[Benefuture]] Currency''' system is a decentralized ecosystem of competing digital currencies on the [[Technology Network]]. Each currency is tied to top-performing [[Measurement|metric models]] in relevant and overlapping [[Domain|domains]] and competes via generated [[Understanding]] and [[Happiness]] per resource consumed. Unlike conventional currency (claims based on market value), Benefuture currency represents claims based on measured or predicted [[Goals|Value]] generation via [[Economy|resource consumption]]. It's the principal means of enacting the benefuture [[Economy]] and nudging aspiring [[Sophont|Sophonts]] to rational [[Benefuture goals|goal]] orientation. Benefuture is not a post-scarcity framework: it is a framework to discover and diminish scarcities.
==Principles==
'''The currency life cycle:'''

* '''[[Good]] happens''' (understanding or happiness increases): Currency generated, distributed via [[#Causal Distribution|causal contribution]].
* '''[[Harm]] happens''': Currency destroyed; reparation systems engage.
* '''Transaction occurs''': Currency moves between [[Entity|entities]].
* '''Resource/time consumed''': Currency paid to [[Reality]] (removed from circulation).

For [[Sophont|participants/users]]:

* [[Output|Outputs]] generating good → pro-rated currency (front-loaded on prediction, back-loaded on measurement), with [[Diversity|novelty]] bonus.
* Meeting baseline needs → priced at or below zero ([[Benesophont#Aspects|sophont baseline]]).
* Outputs causing harm → currency deducted (also from [[Administration]] if systemic failure).
* Experiencing harm → reparation from responsible parties.
* Accumulation beyond needs threshold → excess is redirected into reinvestment in that [[Domain]]; the reinvestment earns [[Reputation]] only through the ordinary channels, if its measured outcomes do good. Overflow itself confers no reputation.

==Aspects==

* '''Competing Currencies''': Multiple currencies based on different [[Measurement|metric model]] sets compete on resource efficiency as Value (understanding/happiness/novelty) generated per physical resource unit.<ref>Hayek, F. A. (1976). ''Denationalisation of Money''. Institute of Economic Affairs. https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1164980W</ref>

* '''No Direct Exchange''': Currencies convert only through real cross-domain actions, not trading. This prevents arbitrage, speculation, and emergence of a meta-currency that would reintroduce centralization.<ref name="Hanson2013">Hanson, R. (2013). Shall we vote on values, but bet on beliefs? ''Journal of Political Philosophy'', ''21''(2), 151-178. https://doi.org/10.1111/jopp.12008</ref>

* '''Implicit Exchange''': "Exchange rates" emerge from resource and transaction markets in overlapping [[Domain|domains]] —currencies that require less resources per unit [[outcome]] are effectively more valuable, but always grounded in real impact.

* '''Probabilistic Escrow''': Networks hold reserves proportional to prediction uncertainty: the upfront follows the [[Measurement|metric model's]] credence that the action's assessed value is positive. Consistent overestimation increases escrow requirements; underestimation decreases them. Escrow ratchets with the pattern of the actor's [[Reputation|ledgers]]: each overstatement uncovered brings more scrutiny. Funds release when back-loaded measurements arrive from all relevant currencies, and payment is settled against the [[Measurement#Assessment|assessed]] difference the action made compared to not acting, predicted at first and measured as outcomes arrive.<ref>Attempts to formalize prediction-contingent payment appear in prediction market literature; see Hanson, R. (2003). Combinatorial information market design. ''Information Systems Frontiers'', ''5''(1), 107-119. https://doi.org/10.1023/a:1022058209073</ref>

* '''[[Diversity|Novelty]] Bonus''': New actions/currencies receive temporary front-loaded boost. Semantic analysis detects superficial variation exploits.

* '''Resource Accounting''': All transactions record resource consumption as physical vectors — quantities in native units (litres, joules, hours, grams) including [[Technology Network|network]] and compute usage (which penalizes overly complex models). Each currency network's [[Measurement|metric models]] independently value these physical resources as domain-specific opportunity costs: what understanding/happiness those resources could have generated in their best alternative use within that domain. The same physical resources carry different opportunity costs in different currencies. Non-renewable and polluting resources carry higher opportunity costs across all domains. Time is valued at foregone understanding/happiness potential, varying by skills and [[Reputation]]. Model compute and network use are resources of the transaction like any other: there is no protocol fee, and a model needing more compute for the same value makes its currency less competitive. Resource accounting is a universal balance sheet: it calculates resource efficiency and adjusts thresholds to maintain the commons and baseline rates, funding both freedom (the sophont baseline) and insurance.
* '''The commons and insurance''': The commons is funded by resource accounting and escrows. The framework is the insurer: the [[Administration]] repairs harm no contributor can be found for, from the commons, to the extent of its capability. Insurance cannot be a business, or it becomes a mechanism of arbitrage.
* '''Behavioural transactions''': see [[System Design#Behavioral Transaction Substrate|System design]].

* '''Embodied understanding/happiness''': Goods embody upstream value (a book embodies author's understanding); price reflects this plus physical resources, while reader's understanding gain often covers cost.

===Transaction Mechanics===

* '''Pricing''': Each currency network prices every transaction independently through its own top ranked [[Measurement|metric model]] with proven predictions: the network's [[Measurement#Assessment|assessed]] opportunity cost of resources consumed (compared to what those resources could have generated in their best alternative use ''within that network's [[domain]],'' hence suboptimal uses cost more), minus the predicted [[Benefuture currency#Causal Distribution|causal distribution]] credit to the consumer for the given transaction (zero if it can't model that transaction), plus [[Benefuture currency#Aspects|escrow]] hold proportional to prediction uncertainty (full hold, if it can't model the transaction). The same action therefore carries different prices across different currency networks — reflecting each model's competence in evaluating the action's understanding/happiness impact within its domain. When predicted value exceeds resource cost, the consumer's effective price is negative — the system pays them to transact. Baseline goods are priced at zero or negative for all [[person]]s whose use of them is within their freedom, and resource accounting is what guarantees that floor. Networks with weak domain coverage of a transaction type will price it high (resource cost dominates when the model cannot confidently predict value), which correctly signals that a different currency is more appropriate for that transaction.
* '''Cross-Domain Transaction Proposals:''' When a [[Sophont]] acts or a significant event is recorded, ''every'' currency network covering a relevant overlapping [[Domain]] independently generates a [[Benefuture currency#Transaction Mechanics|Transaction Proposal]] for that action. Transactors may compose their proposal from any subset of the offered currency proposals, but escrow settlement always accounts against measurement from the union of all concerned currencies for the action's domains — a composed proposal is a price quote, not a waiver of measured consequences. A physicist buying a meal receives transaction proposals from PhysicsCoin, NutritionCoin, HealthCoin, and any other network whose [[Measurement|metric model]] claims [[domain]] coverage for the action's inputs or outputs. Each network prices the transaction through its own model: NutritionCoin prices the meal cheaply (its model understands nutrition and sees high understanding/happiness per resource unit for feeding a hungry person), while PhysicsCoin prices the same meal expensively (its model has minimal domain overlap with food and cannot accurately assess the nutritional value, so the resource cost dominates).
* '''Tentative''': Before action execution, currency networks provide proactive offers—sophonts evaluate predicted understanding/happiness consequences as credits, debits or escrow before committing.
* '''Completed''': After irreversible action, networks provide reactive offers. Sophont accepts, partially accepts, or disputes. Default: user's established preferences or top-performing network's offer.
* '''Approximate''': When the system faces [[Progressive complexity|imprecise data]], it approximates the transaction with the closest statistical estimates that fit the higher resolution data surrounding it, with a lower predictive confidence reducing the front load of the probabilistic escrow on that transaction.
* '''Disputes''': Wrongful assessment claims trigger review via [[Justice]] system.
* '''Embedded [[Law|Laws]]''': Legal requirements encoded in metric models—lawful behavior incentivized, unlawful penalized automatically through currency debits.

==== Causal Distribution ====
Rewards and penalties flow via causal attribution. An outcome is an event; a metric model's measurement estimates its understanding, happiness and novelty per domain and per being. A positive outcome pays its contributors and a negative outcome penalises them, and the penalties fund the repair. Harm destroys at the being level; harm to a commons affects every being using it.

'''Causal Contribution''': A [0,1] score quantifying an entity's role in outcomes. Metric models trace the [[System Design#Behavioral Transaction Substrate|transaction tree]] backwards, attributing credit weighted by degree of separation and [[Domain]] relevance, and netting what each entity paid onward against what it received, so that along a chain each link is credited for the value it added and not for the volume passing through it - which approximates shapley-esque mechanisms <ref>Shapley, L. S. (1953). A Value for n-Person Games. In H. W. Kuhn & A. W. Tucker (Eds.), ''Contributions to the Theory of Games'' (Vol. 2, pp. 307-317). Princeton University Press. https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400881970-018</ref> Where causal explanation unavailable, fallback to statistical traversal.

'''Causal Distribution''': Converts causal contribution into currency flows and [[Reputation]] changes, proportional to contribution plus contribution type novelty bonus. Distribution is net adjustment over front-loaded probabilistic escrow payments, preventing double-counting between predicted and measured value. Disputes resolved via [[Justice]].

This allows indirect, infrastructural, and temporally distant contributions, which are invisible in conventional systems, to receive appropriate credit and incentive.

=== Anti-Manipulation ===
The system incorporates [[Adversarial adaptation]] and [[Anti-Corruption]] principles in its [[System Design]]:

* '''Resource costs''': Every transaction costs resources (paid to Reality), preventing costless cycling.
* '''Escrow''': Low/negative real understanding/happiness transactions face escrow requirements exceeding potential gain.
* '''Novelty decay''': Repetitive/artificial transactions lose bonus rapidly.
* '''Anomaly detection''': [[Technology Network]] flags suspicious patterns.
* '''Reputation penalties''': Gaming attempts risk reputation damage exceeding potential currency gain.
* '''Back-loaded adjustment''': Ultimate rewards depend on measured outcomes, not predictions alone.
* '''Model evolution''': Metric models continuously update; gaming strategies that exploit current models become visible as models improve.

Lossy conversion through repeated exchanges remains theoretically possible but practically unprofitable given cumulative inefficiencies and active detection.
==Examples==
'''Learning a Skill:'''

A sophont learns a programming language. Competing offers:

* ''EduCoin'': 50 units front-loaded; 100 on course completion; 1/month for year if skill generates understanding/happiness.
* ''InnoCur'': 80 front-loaded (novelty bonus); 20 on completion.
* ''ResiCoin'': 20 front-loaded; 5/month for five years if demonstrable long-term impact.

Sophont chooses mix based on risk preference and time horizon.

'''Food Chain:'''

*A farmer grows grain and sells to a miller, who makes flour and sells to a baker, who makes bread bought and eaten by a foodie.
**  Farmer: Debited AgriCoin to [[Reality]] for resource use (land, water, fertilizer). Credited AgriCoin (front-loaded) for producing food. Further credited (back-loaded) based on grain quality and its eventual use (successful consumption, waste, etc.).
**   Miller: Debited FoodCoin to [[Reality]] for energy use. Credited FoodCoin (front & back-loaded) for adding value by processing.
**   Baker: Debited FoodCoin to [[Reality]] for energy. Credited FoodCoin (front & back-loaded) for baking.
**   Foodie: Pays FoodCoin to the baker for the bread. If the foodie eats the bread, they receive a small back-loaded FoodCoin and NutriCoin credit. If the foodie lets the bread spoil, debit FoodCoin (waste).
** If burnt by the baker, the baker takes a significant FoodCoin penalty, and previous parties get minimal backloaded reward.
**   If the foodie gets sick from contaminated bread, HealthCoin is involved:
***   Foodie: Experiences temporary illness (-10 HealthCoin) and recovery (+10 HealthCoin).
***   Baker: Debited HealthCoin (responsibility).
***   Medical experts: Credited HealthCoin (front-loaded for treatment, back-loaded for successful outcome AND for data collection, generating understanding).
*   [[Administration]]: May be debited HealthCoin (systemic failure). The understanding gain from medical data partially offsets treatment costs.

'''Harmful Action:'''

Sophont knowingly posts a falsehood, or keeps one up after a correction has been offered. InfoCoin debits 60 units; honest error carries no debit, at most a wider escrow on the claim, since debate with error keeps truth alive. Options:

* Accept debit.
* Partially accept; seek alternative (e.g., DebateCoin offers smaller debit if sophont successfully engages in structured debate).
* Dispute assessment, triggering [[Justice]] review.

Default if no response: primary network debits automatically.

'''Selective composition:''' a logger felling a stand receives proposals from woodcoin (+80, timber), craftcoin (+25, artisan supply), and ecocoin (−60, watershed and habitat over a long horizon), and composes only the first two. The quote reads +105, but settlement draws on all three: the upfront is scaled toward the union's +45, with the optimistic remainder held in escrow against realized watershed measurement. Had the logger composed ecocoin in, its price signal while harvesting selectively and sparing the riparian strip, it would have cut the predicted harm to −15 and netted +90 with a smaller hold: listening to the harm-predictor pays better, before any harm occurs. The discipline is symmetric — if ecocoin's forecast proves overcautious, settlement releases more escrow back and its accuracy weighting decays accordingly.

===Example currencies===

*'''Agricoin:''' Incentivizes sustainable agriculture practices, efficient resource use, and the production of high-quality, nutritious food, pays to reality upon production, front-loads rewards with expected food production value, back-loads decreasingly upon food processing, distribution and consumption.
*'''FoodCoin:''' Promotes access to healthy and delicious food, and disincentivizes food waste.
*'''HealthCoin:''' Rewards actions that improve physical and mental well-being, including preventative care, early detection of illness, and the development of new treatments. Tracks and records temporary harm for future learning. Initial cost on ill health symptoms, recuperations on treatment and recovery.
*'''MetalCoin:''' Incentivizes responsible mining and metal production, with a focus on resource extraction, efficiency, material durability and recycling.
*'''ToolCoin:''' Focuses on the creation, distribution, and use of high-quality, long-lasting tools.
*'''HelpCoin:''' Rewards acts of kindness, cooperation, community support, and mutual aid.
* '''StandCoin:''' Unspecialized root-level currency for work no qualified currency can price. It has no value predictor, which would contradict incommensurability: it charges only resource cost, by availability, so its work is priced high. The work is still predicted and screened by the best available model; it halts only if the proposer cannot pay or gather other experts to co-fund it, and co-funders are credited through causal distribution. An outcome no domain can price is a surprise that drives investigation and research.
*'''EduCoin:''' Encourages and rewards teaching, mentorship, and the distribution of knowledge, front loads on teaching, back loads on students doing well.
*'''InnoCur:''' incentivizes the creation and sharing of novel, and useful things. Front-loads on novel ideas, backloads on successful implementation.
*'''ResiCoin:''' Encourages long term beneficial thinking, front loads on [[good]] [[Contract|commitments]] and back loads on success or clawbacks on failure to complete them.
*'''VeriCoin:''' Incentivizes [[truth]], fact-checking, and the identification and correction of misinformation. Front-loads on expected truth, back-loads upon [[proof]].

==Discussion==
The multi-currency design addresses Goodhart's Law<ref>Strathern, M. (1997). 'Improving ratings': Audit in the British University system. ''European Review'', ''5''(3), 305-321. https://doi.org/10.1002/(sici)1234-981x(199707)5:3%3C305::aid-euro184%3E3.0.co;2-4</ref>: when any single measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Competing currencies with competing metrics prevent capture of "the" definition of value. Currencies that game their own metrics lose to currencies with more accurate models.

The approach parallels capability theory in welfare economics<ref>Sen, A. (1999). ''Development as Freedom''. Oxford University Press. https://openlibrary.org/works/OL116534W</ref><ref>Nussbaum, M. C. (2011). ''Creating Capabilities: The Human Development Approach''. Harvard University Press. https://doi.org/10.4159/harvard.9780674061200</ref>: distinct value dimensions measured separately rather than collapsed into single utility. Domain-locking preserves this pluralism against financialization pressure.

Hayek's knowledge problem<ref>Hayek, F. A. (1945). The Use of Knowledge in Society. ''American Economic Review'', 35(4), 519–530.</ref> holds that prices aggregate dispersed, tacit knowledge no planner can collect. Benefuture prices are not planned: each network's metric model quotes independently, sophonts' composition of proposals is revealed preference fed back as transaction markers, and escrow settles quotes against outcomes. The discovery process<ref>Hayek, F. A. (1968/2002). Competition as a Discovery Procedure. ''Quarterly Journal of Austrian Economics'', 5(3), 9–23. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12113-002-1029-0</ref> runs among competing models rather than among goods prices. Whether model competition aggregates tacit knowledge as well as prices do is a conjecture for simulation.

===== Supply Stability =====
Currency supply is endogenous — directly coupled to net measured [[Good]] minus [[Benefuture currency#Aspects|resource consumption]] minus [[Harm]]. No lending multiplier, no central authority creation, no fiat expansion. This eliminates the primary mechanisms of inflation and deflation that afflict conventional currencies: decoupling of money supply from real value. Under reasonably accurate resource accounting, inflation requires more Good to be measured than resources consumed — which reflects genuine productivity surplus, the one scenario where currency expansion is appropriate. Deflation requires the reverse — genuine contraction. Several mechanisms act as automatic stabilisers. [[Benefuture currency#Aspects|Probabilistic escrow]] throttles currency creation under uncertainty: overestimating models face ratcheting escrow requirements, acting as an automatic anti-inflation brake. [[Benefuture currency#Principles|Balance overflow]] prevents hoarding-driven deflation by imposing an automatic velocity floor — currency cannot accumulate beyond threshold without being redirected into [[Domain]]-specific reinvestment which absorbs currency through activity. Competition between currencies provides a natural hedge: if one currency inflates due to overgenerous metrics, users migrate to tighter alternatives, reducing the inflating currency's effective value. [[Benesophont#Aspects|Sophont baseline]] acts as an automatic countercyclical stabiliser, maintaining minimum demand during contraction. Residual risks include transient inflationary pulses from genuine productivity breakthroughs (self-correcting as the domain expands to absorb new capacity) and measurement lag on long-horizon activities where escrow holds lock currency during extended feedback loops. Gradual escrow release as interim measurements arrive — rather than all-or-nothing release at a single endpoint — reduces the lag to tolerable drag. These represent friction and drag inherent to coupling currency to real value, which is the cost of preventing the power concentration that fully fungible currency enables.

'''Tradeoffs acknowledged''':

'''Complexity cost:''' Multiple currencies require [[Progressive complexity|progressive]] interface design to prevent choice overload.

'''Bootstrap dependence:''' Initial metric models inherit current understanding's biases; evolutionary pressure corrects but cannot eliminate founding assumptions.

'''Threshold calibration:''' Accumulation thresholds, escrow ratios, novelty decay rates require tuning; [[Government|Benefuture democracy]] provides governance mechanism for contested parameters.

'''Cross-domain friction:''' The no-direct-exchange rule imposes a real cost: transactions outside a [[Sophont]]'s primary production [[Domain]] are more expensive in production-domain currency than in consumption-domain currency (a singer pays less for a microphone than an astrophysicist, the same singer pays much more for a telescope than the astrophysicist). This is the price of preventing wealth-to-political-power conversion via full fungibility. The friction stays manageable because most human activity is genuinely multi-domain (a meal simultaneously touches Agriculture, Health, Culture, and Social domains), [[Benesophont#Aspects|sophont baseline]] covers fundamental cross-domain needs, and the price differential itself serves as a correct information signal directing sophonts toward currencies whose models best understand their transactions.
