Communication for an [[entity]] (communicator) is to produce [[output]]s specifically with the aim of passing specific information to or from another [[entity]] (communicatee) which is trying to [[understanding|understand]] the specific information.

==Aspects==
* Successful communication is theoretically achieved when the communicatee's [[understanding]] of the passed information is the same as the communicator's understanding of the information.
* It is nearly impossible to ascertain successful communication, but sufficient success can be ascertained when the communicatee's description and predictions about the specific information are sufficiently similar to the communicator's.
* Usually communication depends on a language, established via precursors which mean the same thing to both entities in question.

==Example==
* Outputing patterns with specific pre-agreed meanings.
* Speaking, writing, signing to somebody in a common language.
