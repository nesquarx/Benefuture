# Open problems shared with every institution

<!-- Moved from README.md (2026-11-06) to keep the README short; refactored (2026-11-18) to tag logical intractability, remove resolved items, relocate evaluation/harness items to docs/EVAL_BACKLOG.md, and link actual Bf design flaws to spikes in docs/PLAN.md. -->

Some results below are failures. They are listed on purpose, and they should be read with one fact
in mind: **none of these problems has been solved by any existing institution either.** They are the
hard edges of any system that relies on measurement, identity and reputation: markets, courts,
audits, elections, peer review, online platforms. Most institutions handle them implicitly. Here each
one is stated as a theorem or a measured finding, with:

1. **What is logically intractable** (mathematical impossibility theorems, information-theoretic limits, or philosophical trade-offs that no institution can eliminate—only bound or explicitly choose which axiom to surrender).
2. **What the design already does** (the friction already built and verified).
3. **What remains open**—separating **missing Bf mechanisms** (in the spec, not yet wired in simulation) from **actual Bf design flaws** (tracked as investigation/fix spikes `D1–D8` in [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md#design-flaw-spikes-d1d8)).

Evaluation-harness limitations, positive stability findings (`8.164`), and untested simulation variants live in [`EVAL_BACKLOG.md`](EVAL_BACKLOG.md). Resolved items (`8.115` closed-world output loss and `8.136` 10-year tie, both flipped to significant wins under `open_entry`; `8.71` normal-credence stalls; `8.133` issuance-cap/withholding probes) are retired from this list and recorded in [`CLAIMS.md`](CLAIMS.md) and [`BACKPROPAGATION.md`](BACKPROPAGATION.md).

| **Deceit that fools every measure at once** (common-mode error) | Shared blind spots across independent checks: in 2008 the major rating agencies rated the same securities AAA ([White 2010](https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.24.2.211)). | **Intractable core (epistemic limit):** before any independent sensor, delayed outcome, or whistleblower diverges, model competition is vacuous. **Missing mechanism:** fixed sim markers omit `↑N(M)` discovery (`L51`) and `SO` (`L142`). **Bf design flaw:** tying domain currency liveness to raw model accuracy (`L82`) lets pre-audit deceit knock out a marginal domain's currency for honest peers. | Escrow settles on measured outcomes and each model prices each actor by its own record with that actor, so in simulation a deceiver that fools every model gains nothing relative to its peers. | **Intermittent ("on-off") deceit** | Reputation systems generally ([Hoffman, Zage & Nita-Rotaru 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1592451.1592452)): good behaviour in between masks bad. | **Intractable core (outputs-only limit):** a one-off long con after a clean record is information-theoretically indistinguishable ex ante (bounded ex post by escrow, clawback, and liability; note `UncollateralizedExploitVulnerability` in [`SPEC_DIVERGENCES.md`](SPEC_DIVERGENCES.md#L175)). **Missing mechanism:** native Deception domain (`8.95`) not yet wired in M3c; statistical precision/recall trade-off between luck protection (`model_z`) and detection speed. | Each deception an audit establishes enters a permanent deception record; its outcomes are discounted from the domain's models (peers 194 against 197 honest, 79 before), escrow rises exponentially in the count (`8.91`), and repeated-pattern sanction escalation (`8.157`) applies. Luck and honest mistakes never raise it. | Only discovered deception counts, so a deceiver acts freely until its first audit; one-off long cons remain out of scope (bounded, not prevented). | **Sybils and false names** | [Douceur 2002](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-45748-8_24) (no open Sybilproofness without an identity anchor); [Cheng & Friedman 2005](https://doi.org/10.1145/1080192.1080202) (no symmetric reputation is Sybilproof); [Aziz et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.3166) (Shapley shares are not false-name-proof). | **Intractable core (3 impossibility theorems):** symmetric reputation and additive/threshold Shapley cannot be Sybilproof without identity binding. **Bf design flaw:** sharp, non-graceful degradation whenever `TN` 1:1 binding leaks, plus symmetric Shapley rewarding pledge-splitting (`8.57-a`) and unbound harm-splitting (`8.58-e`). | Shapley's null-player property gives an idle sybil nothing. Weighted TN binding ([`binding_admits_weighted_spec`](../Benefuture/Systems/TechnologyNetwork.lean)) blocks whitewashing and compromised-cluster `:per_identity` capture in M5 (`P14–P15`, admitting >90% of honest newcomers); multi-hop `propagated_liability` + capped delegation + overflow + AdvAp disperse hub concentration (`0.846 -> 0.246`, `P23`); `bound_shapley_shares` pools bound fork families (`P34`, cutting `8.58-e` harm-split gain `+0.267 -> 0.0`); `fork_split_ref` divides all social power on fork and copied ballots count once. | Unbound harm splitting still lightens a convex penalty by `+0.267` (`8.58-e`), and unbound forks game funding-game Shapley shares (`8.57-a`). Identity binding stays a hard prerequisite. Entry-point binding in the unified world (P57) cuts a sybil army to nothing when no listener is fooled, but at a 2% per-step fooling rate duped vouches seed the ring and the cut is small (ballot share 0.10 to 0.16 against 0.167); tracked fixes: attester-skill weighting, vouch rate limits, flagged vouches counting less, and the cluster-farming attack. Asymmetric/causal Shapley, pledge-proportional funding shares, and bounded `explore_share` (`8.58-a`) are tracked in **Spike `D6`** ([`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md#design-flaw-spikes-d1d8)). |
| **Measurement sets the ceiling** | Every evidence-based institution is only as good as its instruments (Goodhart's Law and control under observation noise). | **Intractable core (epistemic / control limit):** no controller can hold a latent floor with probability 1 under noisy observations, and finite proxy sets face Goodhart pressure. **Missing mechanism:** M1 omits competing chains and audits. **Bf design flaw:** `G` (`L74`) specifies `¬↓min` as a deterministic hard constraint despite noisy measurement. | Models compete and none is protected. Noise ranges are calibrated, outages degrade to coarser fallbacks and are re-measured later, and with perfect measurement the floors provably never fall. Competing measurement chains audit each other on divergence and demote chains that hide harm; a lone chain is demoted only past a sequential significance test; models carry uncertainty into credence. | With realistic noise on current levels alone, the true floor fell in 9% of steps ([M1](../sim/README.md#m1-observations)). | **Population ethics** | [Arrhenius 2000](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266267100000249) proves no welfarist axiology satisfies Repugnant Conclusion avoidance, Non-Sadism, and Mere Addition together. | **Intractable core (impossibility theorem):** every theory must surrender at least one axiom (Bf explicitly gives up Mere Addition). **Fixed (`D1`, Ruled & Promoted 2026-11-19):** defining `min` as a raw quantile (`L74`) without `tail(H < Th_H) → Me` previously surrendered Non-Sadism too in forced additions (`8.56`). | G ranks actions against not acting, not whole worlds. When uplift exists, G never chooses inaction ([`uplift_beats_inaction`](../Benefuture/Proposed/PopulationDirection.lean)), and removing beings cannot raise the floor ([`removal_cannot_raise_floor`](../Benefuture/Proposed/PopulationDirection.lean)). | **Honest forecasters lose to extremisers** | Winner-take-all forecasting contests reward overstated confidence ([Witkowski et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.4410)). | **Intractable edge (rank-contest theorem):** discrete winner-take-all cutoffs on finite samples reward risk-seeking extremisers even under proper scores. **Fixed (`D2`, Ruled & Promoted 2026-11-19):** prior-free `credence_ref` omitted a domain outcome prior, making honest credences systematically underconfident in positive domains (`8.58-c`). | Challengers compete only on full record windows (`8.58-b`), credence comes from Empirical-Bayes record shrinkage (`8.71` + `D2`), and in M5 voting credences weigh only as far as each voter's record proves calibration (a colluding bloc stating maximal confidence on hidden outcomes: decisions 67% right as stated vs. 96% calibrated, probe `8.121`, 6 seeds, re-run green 2026-11-22; a bloc that is merely overconfident, 3x stated log-odds, is harmless on all ballot kinds, see `docs/BACKPROPAGATION.md`). **`D2` promoted to default (`credence_rule = :eb`, `L84` `Mem` prior):** [`eb_credence`](../Benefuture/Proposed/RecordCredence.lean) shrinks predictions toward the `Mem` domain prior weighted by calibrated error variance ([`eb_posterior_mean_between`](../Benefuture/Proposed/RecordCredence.lean), [`eb_posterior_var_strict_lt_errVar`](../Benefuture/Proposed/RecordCredence.lean)), reducing `ext_world` extremist takeover (`6/14` vs `14/18`; not eliminated), closing mid-range underconfidence (`+0.1629 -> -0.0679`), and cutting heavy-tailed side-harm stalls at `z = 3.0` (`P49`). | Rank-contest winner-take-all theory remains inherently fragile when discrete cutoffs replace continuous proper-score credences. |

## Remaining architectural & structural open questions

Each item below names the friction already in place, its intractability/root-cause status, and what remains open.

- **Meta-research priority (`8.131–8.132`, conjecture) — *Intractable VOI regime-dependence + Bf design flaw*.** Friction: knowing which understanding accelerates the goals is itself a domain with a record, and published priorities adopt a meta result only past the significance bar. Open: a small meta share lifts the floor only when a society shares a correlated misjudgement; with independent errors, education alone does the work, and noisy meta results do not lift the floor. Statically hardcoding `meta^x > median > floor` in `L58` is tracked in **Spike `D7`** ([`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md#design-flaw-spikes-d1d8)).
- **Cross-domain resource price discovery under non-fungible currencies (`L83`, `L93`, `8.133`) — *Structural Bf design risk*.** Friction: record credence (`8.71`) removed stalls; issuance caps (`L83`), supply-withholding clawbacks (`8.133`), and the `ra_domain` estimating domain (`P10`, `P11`: online `ra_unit_value`, `ra_alloc_weight`, `ra_commons_reserve`, `ra_spend_contract`) resolve single-pool resource overshoot in probes. Open: forbidding currency exchange (`!trading, !Arbitrage`, `L93`) pushes cross-domain scarcity price discovery across heterogeneous rivalrous physical bottlenecks onto `RA`. Multi-resource stress testing is tracked in **Spike `D8`** ([`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md#design-flaw-spikes-d1d8)).
- **Restraint by judicial panels: expert collusion, alarm to governance (`L115`, `L117`, `L120`, `L121`, `8.170`) — *Structural Bf design question, measured in the toy*.** Friction: restraint is the last-resort verdict of a panel of judicial experts voting with stated confidence, liability for a false restraint sits on the experts who voted for it (judicial-domain reputation first, currency on a repeated pattern), the enforcer answers only for execution excess, audits are commons-funded with recursion within a budget, and an alarm (`restraint_balance_flawed_ref`) flags a process whose false restraints reach its true ones (nominal toy: false/true 0.04, no chill from liability; captured panel: alarm in 24 of 24 seeds, one false alarm in 24 nominal seeds; `sim/battery/results/restraint_balance.txt`). Closed by 8.173: acquittals are audited at a governed random rate and AdvAp-challengeable, wrongful acquittals are charged to the experts who voted to acquit, and the alarm reads both error types (fully captured acquitting panel: alarm in 21 of 24 seeds against 0 of 24, the rest missed because about 7 audited acquittals per run barely reach the minimum findings; partially captured panels with one honest expert fire in only 3 of 8). Remaining open: the alarm's power under partial capture and a low acquittal-audit rate; expert calibration is now estimated from audited convictions and acquittals, still a selected sample; the alarm is a loud trace entry, not yet a governance candidate; experts carry no currency balance, so the repeated-pattern charge is a ledger; collusion of experts with an enforcer and challenge flooding are argued (`sim/README.md`) but not tested; the missed-harm side is only a proxy and audit spend is a toy value.
- **Decorrelated model collusion (`B4`, `8.2`, `8.23`) — *Intractable statistical limit + missing sim coverage*.** Friction: per-(actor, model) track records, record credence, and adversarial adaptation hold escrow within settled value and collapse the proxy gap (re-established at 64 paired seeds, `z ≥ 3`). Open: a cartel deliberately decorrelated in its outputs is statistically hard to detect on small samples; full Bf relies on `AdvAp` insider whistleblowing (`L161`) and `OpenSource` inspection (`L51`), which remain to be simulated against a decorrelated cartel (see [`EVAL_BACKLOG.md`](EVAL_BACKLOG.md)).
