# Open problems shared with every institution

<!-- Moved from README.md (2026-11-06) to keep the README short; links and claims unchanged. -->



Some results below are failures. They are listed on purpose, and they should be read with one fact
in mind: **none of these problems has been solved by any existing institution either.** They are the
hard edges of any system that relies on measurement, identity and reputation: markets, courts,
audits, elections, peer review, online platforms. Most institutions handle them implicitly. Here each
one is stated as a theorem or a measured finding, with the conditions under which it bites, what
already limits the damage, and a tracked candidate fix where one exists. Every item was found by the
project's own adversarial tests, which is evidence the tests have teeth.

In short, a failure listed here marks the current boundary of a design that can be tested, and a
reader can check exactly where that boundary sits.

| Problem | Where it bites outside Benefuture | What the design already does | What remains open |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deceit that fools every measure at once** (common-mode error) | Shared blind spots across independent checks: in 2008 the major rating agencies rated the same securities AAA ([White 2010](https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.24.2.211), on how agency incentives and regulatory reliance converged). | Escrow settles on measured outcomes and each model prices each actor by its own record with that actor, so in simulation a deceiver that fools every model gains nothing relative to its peers. AdvAp reporting recovers part of the damage ([m6_tests](../sim/test/m6_tests.jl)). | Competing models add nothing when all of them share the error; the defence then rests on outcome measurement and on people reporting. Before settlement the harm lands on the domain: in a marginal domain it kept the currency from qualifying in 2 of 6 seeds. |
| **Intermittent ("on-off") deceit** | Reputation systems generally ([Hoffman, Zage & Nita-Rotaru 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1592451.1592452)): good behaviour in between masks bad. | Each deception an audit establishes enters a permanent deception record; its outcomes are discounted from the domain's models (so the fooled records no longer take the currency down for everyone: peers 194 against 197 honest, 79 before) and escrow rises exponentially in the count (author ruling, [8.91](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). Luck and honest mistakes are never established as deception, so they never raise it. | Only discovered deception counts, so a deceiver acts freely until its first audit; a one-off long con after a clean record is out of scope (bounded by escrow, clawback and liability, not caught). A deception domain with its own models is proposed ([8.95](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). |
| **Sybils and false names** | [Douceur 2002](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-45748-8_24) shows that without a trusted identity authority, one actor can always pose as many. [Cheng & Friedman 2005](https://doi.org/10.1145/1080192.1080202) prove that symmetric reputation cannot be sybilproof. | Shapley's null-player property gives a sybil that adds nothing exactly nothing. Identity binding by attesting clusters links whitewashing attempts and keeps model accuracy equivalent to no attack. | Splitting a *harm* across identities lightens its penalty by about 11% ([8.58-e](BACKPROPAGATION.md)), and forks game Shapley shares ([8.57-a](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). Identity binding is therefore a prerequisite, not an optional extra. Sybil *beings* are not yet simulated. |
| **Measurement sets the ceiling** | Every evidence-based institution is only as good as its instruments (Goodhart's law in all its variants). | Models compete and none is protected. Noise ranges are calibrated, outages degrade to coarser fallbacks and are re-measured later, and with perfect measurement the floors provably never fall in simulation. | With realistic noise the guarantee weakens: noise on current levels alone let the true floor fall in 9% of steps ([M1](../sim/README.md#m1-observations)). |
| **Population ethics** | [Arrhenius 2000](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266267100000249) proves that no welfarist axiology can satisfy a short list of individually plausible conditions, so every theory gives one up. | G ranks actions against not acting, not whole worlds. In a probe, with newborns counted in the floors at their predicted level, G refuses additions that lower the floors and takes 10 good lives over 200 barely worth living ([m7_tests](../sim/test/m7_tests.jl)). When an admissible uplift exists, G never chooses to do nothing ([`uplift_beats_inaction`](../Benefuture/Proposed/PopulationDirection.lean)), and removing beings cannot raise the floor ([`removal_cannot_raise_floor`](../Benefuture/Proposed/PopulationDirection.lean)). | Non-Sadism is given up only when an addition is forced: if beings will arrive anyway and G can only redirect them, the quantile floor can prefer a few lives below neutral to many barely positive ones ([8.56](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). By the author's ruling ([8.86](BACKPROPAGATION.md)), a forced addition is taken as given, like a suffering polity joining a Benefuture nation. The work is then to uplift the newcomers. Over the long term no existing individual may end lower; passing harm may be accepted as necessary and absorbed as deliberated; where that cannot be met, G falls back to protecting the floors, and says so. It is shown in the accession probe ([probe_tests](../sim/test/probe_tests.jl), "Probe: accession and uplift (8.86)"). A positive floor alone moves the repugnant conclusion rather than resolving it ([`floor_shifts_repugnant_conclusion`](../Benefuture/Axiology/RepugnantConclusion.lean)). |
| **Rights against necessary harm; doing against allowing** | These are long-standing open questions in moral philosophy, and law settles them case by case. | Freedom is reported per plan, and irreversible harm is excluded. | Freedom excludes irreversible harm even when it is necessary and net-positive ([`freedom_excludes_irreversible_necessary_harm`](../Benefuture/Subgoals/Freedom.lean)). Doing and allowing are not distinguished. |
| **Honest forecasters lose to extremisers** | Winner-take-all forecasting contests reward overstated confidence ([Witkowski et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.4410)). | Challengers compete only on full record windows (8.58-b), and credence comes from the record (8.71). | In the economy, takeovers by equal-skill challengers are still extremists' (9 of 9) because honest credences are underconfident there ([8.58-c](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). |

---

- **Priced-out work is not replaced in closed worlds (8.115).** Benefuture's discovery institutions price out skilled deceivers; in a fixed eight-agent economy their real work is lost (about 10% of growth against status-quo institutions). Tracked fix: entry (newcomers taking unmet demand) in the comparison world, and a variant where the deceivers' work is not useful.

- **Replay does not yet separate Benefuture from the fewest-deaths rule** (E4 round 2, 2026-11-08): both are right on 8 of 9 settled cases. Cases where they differ (German nuclear, Sri Lanka) are few, and the priority tiers decide only where options are close in size but differ in kind, which few cases pose. Next: contrast cases chosen for that, declared from as-of sources.
- **Freedom's pricing of self-regarding risk is not modelled in the replay** (Prohibition 1919): a ban on a chosen risk is weighed only through happiness lost, not through the freedom boundary's preference for pricing over prohibition.
- **Base rates from recorded events only** (swine flu 1976): built: cases declare `[unrecorded_trials]` (with sources) and they count as non-occurrences. On swine flu it does not change the choice even at a third the rate; the open part is the case's pricing of the stockpile option (late vaccine averting a quarter), and verifying the as-of reports before any trials are declared.
- **Contrast cases for priority tiers are hard to build by hand** (2026-11-08): of three, the tiers decided one (NSF 1950); in Head Start the evidence differed, and in tobacco control the expected deaths differed by construction error. Built: `contrast_check` refuses a contrast case whose acting options differ in expected deaths beyond a tolerance (tobacco 1964 refused; NSF and Head Start pass); used before every contrast replay. Still open: equal as-of evidence (Head Start) is not checked, and a contrast isolates the tiers only when both options deliver the same quantity in different kinds (public broadcasting 1967 was decided by understanding's priority over happiness instead).
