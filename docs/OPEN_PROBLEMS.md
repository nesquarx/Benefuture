# Open problems shared with every institution

<!-- Moved from README.md (2026-11-06) to keep the README short; links and claims unchanged. -->



Some results below are failures. They are listed on purpose, and they should be read with one fact
in mind: **none of these problems has been solved by any existing institution either.** They are the
hard edges of any system that relies on measurement, identity and reputation: markets, courts,
audits, elections, peer review, online platforms. Most institutions handle them implicitly. Here each
one is stated as a theorem or a measured finding, with the conditions under which it bites, what
already limits the damage, and a tracked candidate fix where one exists. Every item was found by the
project's own adversarial tests, which is evidence the tests have teeth.

In short, a failure listed here marks the current boundary of a design that can be tested, and a
reader can check exactly where that boundary sits.

| Problem | Where it bites outside Benefuture | What the design already does | What remains open |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deceit that fools every measure at once** (common-mode error) | Shared blind spots across independent checks: in 2008 the major rating agencies rated the same securities AAA ([White 2010](https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.24.2.211), on how agency incentives and regulatory reliance converged). | Escrow settles on measured outcomes and each model prices each actor by its own record with that actor, so in simulation a deceiver that fools every model gains nothing relative to its peers. AdvAp reporting recovers part of the damage ([m6_tests](../sim/test/m6_tests.jl)). | Competing models add nothing when all of them share the error; the defence then rests on outcome measurement and on people reporting. Before settlement the harm lands on the domain: in a marginal domain it kept the currency from qualifying in 2 of 6 seeds. |
| **Intermittent ("on-off") deceit** | Reputation systems generally ([Hoffman, Zage & Nita-Rotaru 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1592451.1592452)): good behaviour in between masks bad. | Each deception an audit establishes enters a permanent deception record; its outcomes are discounted from the domain's models (so the fooled records no longer take the currency down for everyone: peers 194 against 197 honest, 79 before) and escrow rises exponentially in the count (author ruling, [8.91](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). Luck and honest mistakes are never established as deception, so they never raise it. | Only discovered deception counts, so a deceiver acts freely until its first audit; a one-off long con after a clean record is out of scope (bounded by escrow, clawback and liability, not caught). A deception domain with its own models is proposed ([8.95](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). |
| **Sybils and false names** | [Douceur 2002](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-45748-8_24) shows that without a trusted identity authority, one actor can always pose as many. [Cheng & Friedman 2005](https://doi.org/10.1145/1080192.1080202) prove that symmetric reputation cannot be sybilproof. | Shapley's null-player property gives a sybil that adds nothing exactly nothing. Identity binding by attesting clusters links whitewashing attempts and keeps model accuracy equivalent to no attack. | Splitting a *harm* across identities lightens its penalty by about 11% ([8.58-e](BACKPROPAGATION.md)), and forks game Shapley shares ([8.57-a](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). Friction already in place: a fork divides everything it carries (balance, debts, reputation ledgers and records, accumulation allowance, capacity to act, base vote; `fork_split_ref`), so forking cannot double a reputation or an allowance, and a merge carries both records and debts ([probe_tests](../sim/test/probe_tests.jl), identity step 2); copied ballots are counted once by the errors they share. Identity binding stays a prerequisite. Sybil *beings* are not yet simulated. |
| **Measurement sets the ceiling** | Every evidence-based institution is only as good as its instruments (Goodhart's law in all its variants). | Models compete and none is protected. Noise ranges are calibrated, outages degrade to coarser fallbacks and are re-measured later, and with perfect measurement the floors provably never fall in simulation. | With realistic noise the guarantee weakens: noise on current levels alone let the true floor fall in 9% of steps ([M1](../sim/README.md#m1-observations)). Friction: competing measurement chains audit each other on divergence and demote a chain that hides harm; a lone chain is demoted only past a sequential significance test; models carry their uncertainty into credence, so a noisy measure buys less weight. |
| **Population ethics** | [Arrhenius 2000](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266267100000249) proves that no welfarist axiology can satisfy a short list of individually plausible conditions, so every theory gives one up. | G ranks actions against not acting, not whole worlds. In a probe, with newborns counted in the floors at their predicted level, G refuses additions that lower the floors and takes 10 good lives over 200 barely worth living ([m7_tests](../sim/test/m7_tests.jl)). When an admissible uplift exists, G never chooses to do nothing ([`uplift_beats_inaction`](../Benefuture/Proposed/PopulationDirection.lean)), and removing beings cannot raise the floor ([`removal_cannot_raise_floor`](../Benefuture/Proposed/PopulationDirection.lean)). | Non-Sadism is given up only when an addition is forced: if beings will arrive anyway and G can only redirect them, the quantile floor can prefer a few lives below neutral to many barely positive ones ([8.56](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). By the author's ruling ([8.86](BACKPROPAGATION.md)), a forced addition is taken as given, like a suffering polity joining a Benefuture nation. The work is then to uplift the newcomers. Over the long term no existing individual may end lower; passing harm may be accepted as necessary and absorbed as deliberated; where that cannot be met, G falls back to protecting the floors, and says so. It is shown in the accession probe ([probe_tests](../sim/test/probe_tests.jl), "Probe: accession and uplift (8.86)"). A positive floor alone moves the repugnant conclusion rather than resolving it ([`floor_shifts_repugnant_conclusion`](../Benefuture/Axiology/RepugnantConclusion.lean)). |
| **Rights against necessary harm; doing against allowing** | These are long-standing open questions in moral philosophy, and law settles them case by case. | Freedom is reported per plan and irreversible harm is excluded; when every option harms, G first looks for a lateral option, then takes the least net expected harm (8.127); deaths from freely chosen risk are logged and repaired as accidents but do not trigger restriction while the risk is unchanged (8.130). | Freedom excludes irreversible harm even when it is necessary and net-positive ([`freedom_excludes_irreversible_necessary_harm`](../Benefuture/Subgoals/Freedom.lean)). Doing and allowing are not distinguished. |
| **Honest forecasters lose to extremisers** | Winner-take-all forecasting contests reward overstated confidence ([Witkowski et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.4410)). | Challengers compete only on full record windows (8.58-b), and credence comes from the record (8.71). | In the economy, takeovers by equal-skill challengers are still extremists' (9 of 9) because honest credences are underconfident there ([8.58-c](BACKPROPAGATION.md)). Friction: in voting, credences weigh only as far as each voter's record proves them calibrated, so a confident bloc holds 67% against 96% for calibrated ballots; an extremist challenger must still beat the incumbent on a full record window. |

---

Each item below names the friction already in place, then what remains open.

- **Priced-out work is not replaced in closed worlds (8.115).** Friction: deceivers are priced out by their own audited records, not banned, so honest work by the same actors still clears. Open: in a fixed eight-agent economy their real work is lost (about 10% of growth against status-quo institutions). Tracked fix: entry (newcomers taking unmet demand) in the comparison world, and a variant where the deceivers' work is not useful.
- **Stalls and a currency running ahead of its resources (8.71, 8.133).** Friction: the record credence removes the measured stall (0 of 8 seeds against 4–6 of 8 under the normal credence); work no model can price falls to costly StandCoin, logged loudly as a call to build models. Open: global resource accounting (issued claims bound to resource and time capacity, contracting pricing, the commons and spending when claims outrun it) is ruled but not built; price contraction alone did not absorb a stall (6 of 8 → 7 of 8).
- **Replay does not yet separate Benefuture from the fewest-deaths rule** (E4 round 2): both are right on 8 of 9 settled cases. Friction against hindsight: cases are frozen with credences before reading, first results are never replaced, and prospective E5 cases are registered before outcomes. Open: few cases pose options close in size but different in kind, where the priority tiers decide; contrast cases decided 3 of 6 (NSF 1950, Land and Water Conservation Fund 1964, Sure Start 1998).
- **Base rates from recorded events only** (swine flu 1976). Friction: cases declare unrecorded trials (with sources) and they count as non-occurrences. Open: on swine flu it does not change the choice even at a third of the rate; the open part is the case's pricing of the stockpile option, and verifying the as-of reports.
- **Contrast cases for priority tiers are hard to build by hand.** Friction: `contrast_check` refuses a contrast whose acting options differ in expected deaths beyond a tolerance (tobacco 1964 refused). Open: equal as-of evidence (Head Start) is not checked, and a contrast isolates the tiers only when both options deliver the same quantity in different kinds.
- **Meta-research pays only in one regime (8.131–8.132, conjecture).** Friction: knowing which understanding accelerates the goals is itself a domain with a record, and the published priorities adopt a meta result only past the significance bar. Open: a small meta share lifts the floor only when a society shares a misjudgement; with independent errors, education alone does the work, and the bar did not lift the floor under noisy meta results.
