# Conjectures for Simulation

Behavioural claims made by the pseudocode and articles that simulation can test, with a measurable
form, and what Lean already settles. "Lean" marks claims whose *mechanism* is proved for every
admissible parameter point; the sim then tests the dynamics or the empirical inputs the proof assumes.
`Conj_*` names refer to `Benefuture.Systems.Conjectures`.

## B. Missed by the sources: suggested conjectures

| # | Conjecture | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Salami-slicing:** splitting a harm into many sub-threshold pieces does not escape weighting | Significance is threshold-based (L25); Lean showed loss aversion only holds at or above threshold. An adversary could fragment harm below it. |
| B2 | **Pascal's mugging:** fabricated low-probability catastrophe claims cannot block actions | Fr prices P·severity with large irreparable multipliers. Bostrom, "Pascal's Mugging" (2009): tiny probabilities of huge stakes can dominate expected-value reasoning. |
| B3 | **Performative prediction:** predictions that change behaviour still converge | MM predictions set prices and escrow, which change what agents do. Perdomo et al., "Performative Prediction" (2020): when predictions influence outcomes, repeated retraining converges only under stability conditions. |
| B4 | **Model collusion:** diverse-looking models coordinating to Goodhart jointly are detected | Anti-herding targets correlation; a cartel can be decorrelated by design. |
| B5 | **Confidence gaming:** agents cannot understate confidence to keep harm below "established" | Fr turns on confidence; Brier scoring is proper (proved), but strategic misreporting under Fr is a different incentive. |

## [CONJ] 8.131: interior optimum for meta-research share (refuted in first regime)
Hypothesis: with per-being noisy priority estimates, the floor of understanding peaks at an interior `meta_share`. First reading (`run_m7_traits`, things [1.0,0.3,0.1], m 6, 200 steps, weight_σ 1.5, `per_being=true`, 8 paired seeds): floor 1.055 / 1.044 / 1.020 / 0.937 / 0.839 at meta_share 0 / 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.6 / 0.9; true weights 1.068. Monotone falling: independent per-being errors largely average out (cost of noise 0.013), so diverted research costs more than it repairs. The global-error case (one shared estimate, earlier probe: 0.977 → 1.058 with meta-research) is where meta-research pays; correlated error is the regime to test next. First result recorded as is.
Continued (8.132, meta-research as its own domain: outcomes sharpen a published policy estimate, beings take 0.3 of the gap each step). 10 paired seeds, floor of understanding at meta_share 0 / 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.3: independent errors 1.062 / 1.053 / 1.053 / 1.015; fully shared errors 1.012 / 1.054 / 1.049 / 1.017. Under shared misjudgement the optimum is interior (≈0.05, +0.042 over none); under independent errors education alone (pooling to the published mean) recovers most of the loss and meta-research does not pay. Probe: `sim/test/probe_tests.jl` (8.132). Status [CONJ], supported in this regime.
Continued: with heavy uptake noise (per-being Beta dispositions, log-normal per step) and research funded by the published priorities, the readings hold: independent 1.065 / 1.056 / 1.015, shared 1.012 / 1.057 / 1.017 at meta_share 0 / 0.05 / 0.3.
Continued: meta-research as a leaf with its own record (noisy proposals, measured gain, adopted past the significance bar). Shared errors, meta_share 0.05, 10 seeds: exact outcomes floor 1.057 (595 adopted, 0 wrong); proposal noise 0.6, no bar: 1.055 (595 adopted, 297 wrong); noise 0.6 with measurement error 0.5 and bar 1.0: 1.053 (45 adopted, 9 wrong). The bar cuts wrong adoptions from 50% to 20% but does not lift the floor here: once priorities are near right, wrong adoptions cost little. Recorded as is.
