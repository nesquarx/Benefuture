Moreau, Daniel
62 Mill Lane, Northwick
Date of birth: 26/09/1966

My story
Based in Northwick, with a background as a generalist. Around then I spent time as barista with Copper Pot Diner. I switched into care work (1992). Early 1990s, I took a course in food hygiene. In 1994, I did a course in public speaking.
Around then I spent time as council officer with Marlow District Council. I won the safety award at Marlow District Council (late 1990s). C. 2001, I moved up to senior council officer at Marlow District Council. Around then I won the team of the year award at County Records Office.
Around 2013, I switched into software. Around then I joined the allotment society.
At some point I picked up conversational French.
