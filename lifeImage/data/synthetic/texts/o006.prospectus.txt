About us
Stonebridge Rovers Sports Club is a small sports club with about 20 people, founded in 2006.

Our history
In 2006 the sports club was founded in Eastholm.
In 2007 the sports club started with thirty members.
In 2007 the sports club grew to 400 members.
In 2010 the sports club grew to 400 members.
In 2010 the sports club cut our waste by a third.
In 2011 the sports club grew to 400 members.
In 2011 the sports club won the regional cup.
In 2012 the sports club stopped single-use packaging.
In 2013 the sports club moved into a converted shop on the high street.
In 2013 the sports club supported the town carnival.
In 2014 the sports club renovated the changing rooms.
In 2015 the sports club supported the winter coat drive.
In 2015 the sports club started a youth programme with eighty children.
In 2015 the sports club grew to 400 members.
In 2015 the sports club won the regional cup.
In 2016 the sports club planted a hedgerow.
In 2017 the sports club took on three new staff.
In 2017 the sports club filed accounts showing a modest profit.
In 2022 the sports club grew to 250 members.
In 2024 the sports club won the county league.
In 2025 the sports club took on two new staff.
In 2025 the sports club won the regional cup.
In 2026 the sports club took on two new staff.

Risks and incidents
We have had no recorded incident that we consider material.

Governance
The organisation is run by a small board and reports to its members once a year.
