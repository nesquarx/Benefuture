About us
Lantern Table Farm is a small farm with about 20 people, founded in 2009.

Our history
In 2009 the farm was founded in Oakhurst.
In 2010 the farm grew the herd to sixty animals.
In 2011 the farm harvested a good barley crop despite the dry spring.
In 2013 the farm grew the herd to ninety animals.
In 2013 the farm filed accounts showing steady turnover.
In 2013 the farm harvested twelve hundred litres of honey.
In 2013 the farm filed accounts showing break-even.
In 2013 the farm harvested a record crop of apples.
In 2014 the farm supported the neighbourhood clean-up.
In 2015 the farm harvested twelve hundred litres of honey.
In 2016 the farm harvested a record crop of apples.
In 2016 the farm filed accounts showing a modest profit.
In 2016 the farm supported the local school fair.
In 2017 the farm trained one apprentices.
In 2019 the farm grew the herd to ninety animals.
In 2020 the farm harvested forty tonnes of potatoes.
In 2021 the farm harvested a record crop of apples.
In 2021 the farm opened a farm shop.
In 2021 the farm harvested a good barley crop despite the dry spring.
In 2024 the farm harvested twelve hundred litres of honey.
In 2024 the farm took on five new staff.
In 2026 the farm grew the herd to sixty animals.
In 2026 the farm took on two new staff.
In 2026 the farm supported the town carnival.

Risks and incidents
We have had no recorded incident that we consider material.

Governance
The organisation is run by a small board and reports to its owners once a year.
