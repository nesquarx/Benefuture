About us
The Copper Gate Cafe is a small cafe with about 3 people, founded in 2011.

Our history
In 2011 the cafe was founded in Stonebridge.
In 2011 the cafe opened to the public.
In 2013 the cafe supported the street party.
In 2014 the cafe stopped single-use packaging.
In 2015 the cafe took on three new staff.
In 2015 the cafe served about 2,000 customers a week.
In 2016 the cafe filed accounts showing steady turnover.
In 2018 the cafe served about 2,000 customers a week.
In 2019 the cafe took on four new staff.
In 2020 the cafe took on two new staff.
In 2020 the cafe served about 800 customers a week.
In 2021 the cafe filed accounts showing a small surplus.
In 2021 the cafe filed accounts showing steady turnover.
In 2022 the cafe served about 400 customers a week.
In 2024 the cafe took on three new staff.
In 2024 the cafe filed accounts showing break-even.
In 2024 the cafe filed accounts showing a modest profit.
In 2025 the cafe served about 400 customers a week.
In 2025 the cafe filed accounts showing break-even.
In 2025 the cafe trained our baristas in latte art.
In 2025 the cafe cut our waste by a third.

Risks and incidents
We have had no recorded incident that we consider material.

Governance
The organisation is run by a small board and reports to its owners once a year.
