About us
Marlowe Bay Food Bank is a small charity with about 5 people, founded in 2002.

Our history
In 2002 the charity was founded in Stonebridge.
In 2003 the charity was registered as a charity.
In 2004 the charity distributed about 1,500 meals a month.
In 2004 the charity filed accounts showing a small surplus.
In 2005 the charity recruited ten regular volunteers.
In 2005 the charity took on four new staff.
In 2005 the charity recruited twenty regular volunteers.
In 2007 the charity had its accounts independently examined.
In 2008 the charity distributed about 800 meals a month.
In 2008 the charity recruited forty regular volunteers.
In 2008 the charity planted a hedgerow.
In 2008 the charity distributed about 800 meals a month.
In 2010 the charity moved into a unit near the station.
In 2010 the charity cut our waste by a third.
In 2010 the charity recruited forty regular volunteers.
In 2011 the charity had its accounts independently examined.
In 2012 the charity supported the town carnival.
In 2013 the charity distributed about 300 meals a month.
In 2013 the charity filed accounts showing steady turnover.
In 2014 the charity took on five new staff.

In 2016 the charity recruited forty regular volunteers.

In 2018 the charity recruited twenty regular volunteers.

In 2019 the charity had its accounts independently examined.

Risks and incidents
We have had no recorded incident that we consider material.

Governance
The organisation is run by a small board and reports to its members once a year.
