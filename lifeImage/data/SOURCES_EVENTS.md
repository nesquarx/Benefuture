# Open data sources for event evidence (prototype research)

Researched 2026-11-21. "Verified" = the licence or terms statement was read on a primary page during this research
(yes), or is from background knowledge only and still to verify (no). Nothing was downloaded. Pages that returned
403, 404 or were blocked are listed under Blocked. Counts are those the primary page stated.

Tiers follow DESIGN.md: claimed (self-report), attested (third party issues or records it), measured (logs,
publications, outputs). Rows from a registry or encyclopedia are attested; self-edited profiles are claimed.

## Blocked or unconfirmed

- Network blocks (403 or unreachable): Nobel API and terms, congress.gov, PSID, Baseball Databank GitHub page, DBLP FAQ (bot protection), lifelogging.eu (host not allowed).
- Licence not stated on the page read: OpenAlex (check `LICENSE.txt` in the bucket), Open Library, GH Archive, Voteview, ATUS, MTUS, NLSY, PSID.
- ORCID's separate Public Data File Use Policy was not read.

## Recommendation: three prototype tracks

### 1. Public lives (notable people and organisations)

1. Wikidata (CC0, verified) as the spine: births, deaths, education, offices, awards, memberships, creative works, with start and end dates. Pair with Wikipedia biographies (CC BY-SA 4.0, verified) as the text a basic model reads for the claimed-to-attested path.
2. MusicBrainz core (CC0, verified) for credits and roles in music; SEC EDGAR for organisations and officers.
3. Nobel laureates as a small gold set once the licence is confirmed.
Restrict to deceased or clearly public figures; keep a deletion path.

### 2. Measured-tier professional records

1. OpenAlex (licence to confirm in `LICENSE.txt`) for works, authors, institutions and its topic tree as a basemap seed; ORCID (CC0 verified, use policy to read) as the attested link between a person and their works. DBLP as a cross-check.
2. SEC EDGAR filings for officers and insiders (attested by law).
3. CourtListener (public domain mark, verified) for judges' disclosures and the negative channel, institutions and judges only.
Use small author samples and show disambiguation confidence.

### 3. Everyday life (anonymised or synthetic)

1. American Time Use Survey (US federal microdata, free; licence statement to confirm) for activity-by-time diaries across every life area; map activity codes to the domain graph (ICATUS-style).
2. Synthea (Apache 2.0, verified) for synthetic health histories with full timelines; extend with a generator for family, work and civic events.
3. MTUS (free registration, terms to read) for cross-country diaries.
Avoid resume datasets of unknown provenance, Bluesky, Mastodon, GitHub handles and IMDb for anything published.
