# Task: relate

You judge how two domains of human life and knowledge relate. Each task gives `input.card` (the domain) and
`input.candidates` (one or more other domains). Use only what the cards say.

Verdicts (exactly one string):

- `same`: the card and the candidate name one domain (synonyms, translations, different granularity of one thing).
- `overlapping`: they share a substantial part but each has a part the other lacks.
- `different`: any overlap is incidental.

Give a one-line reason that cites the labels or anchors you used. Do not use outside knowledge of the sources.
