# Task: axes

You place a domain of human life on two axes. Each task gives `input.card`: a domain's id, label, description and
the labels of its sectors. Domains cover all of life (care, home, faith, play, harm, work, science), not only work.

Axis 1, abstract (−) to tangible (+): is the domain mainly about ideas, symbols, meaning and minds (−), or bodies,
objects, materials, places and physical acts (+)?

Axis 2, personal and social (−) to global and professional (+): is it mainly lived in the self, family, friends and
local community (−), or in institutions, professions, markets, states and the world at large (+)?

Score each axis −2, −1, 0, +1 or +2, with the extremes kept for the poles:

- Give ±2 only when the domain is clearly at that pole: almost all of it, for nearly everyone, lies on that side.
- When the domain is mixed, leaning one way but with a real share on the other side, give ±1.
- Give 0 when it is balanced, or genuinely both.

Most domains are not at a pole on both axes; expect ±2 to be the minority of your scores, and check each ±2 against
the question "is there a substantial part of this domain on the other side?" (if so, lower it). Judge the domain as
most people live it, not its academic study, unless the domain is itself a field of study.

The verdict is the two scores as "A,B", for example "-2,+1" (axis 1 first, signs always written except for 0).

Reason: one line naming what decided each score.
